The Blacksmith, **Oswald Anders**, is the first craftsperson you rescue. Follow **Find The Sleeping Survivor** to the Ancient Vault - Blacksmith, not far from the Cinder Vault starting area, awaken him, then summon him inside your Flame Altar area.

## Why rescue the Blacksmith first?

Oswald opens the central metalworking progression:

- Melee armor, weapons, and shields
- Improved pickaxes, axes, and rakes
- Charcoal Kiln
- Forge
- Smelter
- Smithing Tools
- Blast Furnace

His quests also lead toward other survivors and later metal tiers.

## Rescue checklist

1. Accept **Find The Sleeping Survivor** during the opening progression.
2. Follow its map marker to Ancient Vault - Blacksmith.
3. Clear the vault approach and interact with Oswald's Cinder Vessel.
4. Return to a base inside an active Flame Altar area.
5. Craft a Summoning Staff and place Oswald.
6. Give him a sheltered room when his quest requests it.

## Best early follow-up

Build the Charcoal Kiln and Forge chain, then complete Blacksmith quests as new regions open. The Crucible recovered through **Crucible Needed For A Smelter** unlocks the Smelter needed for [Copper Bars](/resources/copper-ore).
