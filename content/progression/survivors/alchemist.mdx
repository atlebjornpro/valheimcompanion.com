The Alchemist, **Balthazar**, is rescued from Ancient Vault - Alchemist during **Powerful Alchemy**. Follow the quest marker, awaken him, then summon and shelter him at your base.

## What the Alchemist unlocks

Balthazar supports magic, consumables, and processed ingredients:

- Potions and spells
- Caster-focused armor
- Mortar
- Grinding Stones
- Alchemy Station
- Laboratory
- Mill

His quest line recovers the Mortar, Cauldron, Scientific Instruments, and other equipment that expands the available recipes.

## Rescue checklist

1. Progress the foundational survivor quests until **Powerful Alchemy** appears.
2. Follow the marker to Ancient Vault - Alchemist.
3. Prepare for the Shroud and bring a Grappling Hook.
4. Awaken Balthazar and return to a Flame-protected base.
5. Summon him and build a sheltered room with a bed when requested.

## Best early follow-up

Complete **The Alchemist's Mortar** to expand early spell and reagent crafting. If playing a magic character, pair his progression with the [Wizard Healer build](/guides/builds/wizard-healer).
