The Hunter, **Athalan Skree**, is rescued from Ancient Vault - Hunter during **Hunter Becomes The Hunted**. Follow the quest marker, awaken her in the vault, then summon her at a protected base.

## What the Hunter unlocks

Athalan is the ranged and survival craftsperson. Her progression includes:

- Arrows and ranged armor
- Drying Rack
- Hand Spindle
- Tanning Station
- Spinning Wheel
- Loom
- Spinning Machine

These stations turn hides and fibers into Dried Fur, Leather, Linen, and other equipment materials.

## Rescue checklist

1. Progress the foundational survivor quests until **Hunter Becomes The Hunted** appears.
2. Follow its marker to Ancient Vault - Hunter.
3. Bring a Grappling Hook, basic healing, and repaired equipment.
4. Awaken Athalan and return to your base.
5. Place her with the Summoning Staff and provide shelter.

## Best early follow-up

Athalan's first quests include **Salt Mine Location**, which marks a major deposit at Egerton Salt Mines. Salt powers Dried Fur and later Leather production, so complete that route before attempting a large armor batch.

See [Where to find Salt](/resources/salt) and the [Ranger Bow build](/guides/builds/ranger-bow).
