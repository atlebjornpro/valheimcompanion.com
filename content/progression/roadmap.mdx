Valheim's progression is really three tracks moving together: biomes, bosses, and crafting stations. Beating a boss usually unlocks the station or item that lets you survive the next biome, which unlocks the next boss. This page is the map connecting all three — see the [world guide](/world) and [boss guide](/bosses) for the depth on each stop.

## The full sequence

| Stage | Biome | Boss | Key unlock |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Meadows](/world/regions/meadows) | [Eikthyr](/bosses/eikthyr) | Antler Pickaxe (mining) |
| 2 | [Black Forest](/world/regions/black-forest) | [The Elder](/bosses/the-elder) | Swamp Key |
| 3 | [Swamp](/world/regions/swamp) | [Bonemass](/bosses/bonemass) | Wishbone (find Silver) |
| 4 | [Mountain](/world/regions/mountain) | [Moder](/bosses/moder) | Dragon Tears → Artisan Table |
| 5 | [Plains](/world/regions/plains) | [Yagluth](/bosses/yagluth) | Black Metal, elemental resistance |
| 6 | [Mistlands](/world/regions/mistlands) | [The Queen](/bosses/the-queen) | Eitr, Majestic Carapace |
| 7 | [Ashlands](/world/regions/ashlands) | [Fader](/bosses/fader) | Flametal, Stone Portal |

## Crafting stations alongside biome progress

The biome/boss ladder above is only half the picture — most of what makes each biome survivable comes from a crafting station built well before you get there.

**Workbench** is the very first station (10 Wood), and almost everything else needs to be built within its range. Once you've mined your first Copper and made Coal, a **Forge** unlocks Bronze-tier gear — the baseline for surviving Black Forest. A **Stonecutter**, unlocked with your first Iron from Swamp crypts, opens stone building, which is far more durable than wood. A **Smelter** and **Charcoal Kiln** round out the early metalworking chain — ore in, bars out, wood in, coal out.

The Workbench and Forge both have their own upgrade paths — a Chopping Block, Tanning Rack, Adze, and Tool Shelf raise the Workbench through four levels; the Forge has a longer six-piece upgrade chain (Anvil, Forge Cooler, Bellows, Grinding Wheel, Smith's Anvil, Forge Toolrack). Each step unlocks higher-tier recipes without building a second station from scratch.

The next real gate is **Moder**. Her drop, Dragon Tears, builds the **Artisan Table**, which unlocks three sub-stations: the **Spinning Wheel** (Flax → Linen Thread, for Padded Armor), the **Windmill** (Barley → Flour, for Bread and pies), and the **Blast Furnace** (smelts Black Metal Scrap into bars, and later Flametal Ore once you reach Ashlands). Black Metal from the Blast Furnace is a hard prerequisite for the Mistlands-tier stations that follow.

In Mistlands, Black Cores from Infested Mines unlock three more stations: the **Black Forge** (Carapace armor, the Mistwalker sword), the **Eitr Refinery** (turns Sap and Softtissue into Refined Eitr), and the **Galdr Table** (magic gear and staves, which also needs Refined Eitr from the Refinery). The practical build order is Moder → Artisan Table → Blast Furnace → farm Black Metal and reach Mistlands → Black Forge / Eitr Refinery → Galdr Table.

## The ore problem

Standard wood portals can't carry raw ore, metal bars, or a handful of special items like Dragon Eggs — only refined, crafted goods pass through. This shapes a lot of early-to-mid-game planning: rather than hauling ore back to a main base, most players smelt on-site near the ore source and portal the finished bars home instead, or build a small forward camp with its own Workbench right at the deposit. Ashlands finally breaks this rule with the **Stone Portal**, built at a Stonecutter, which can carry ore and metal — a genuine quality-of-life shift for anyone still hauling cargo by boat at that point.

## A rough order of operations

1. Build a Workbench and basic shelter in **Meadows**, then clear nearby Greydwarf nests and summon [Eikthyr](/bosses/eikthyr).
2. Push into **Black Forest** for Copper and Tin, build a Forge, gear up in Bronze, and take down [The Elder](/bosses/the-elder) for the Swamp Key.
3. Clear **Sunken Crypts** in the Swamp for Iron, build a Stonecutter, and fight [Bonemass](/bosses/bonemass) for the Wishbone.
4. Head into **Mountain** with Frost Resistance, mine Silver using the Wishbone, and beat [Moder](/bosses/moder) — then build the Artisan Table and Blast Furnace immediately, since everything downstream needs Black Metal.
5. Farm Black Metal from **Plains** Fulings, gear up in Padded Armor, and take on [Yagluth](/bosses/yagluth) with Fire Resistance Mead ready.
6. Push into **Mistlands** for Black Cores, build the Black Forge/Eitr Refinery/Galdr Table chain, and fight [The Queen](/bosses/the-queen) in the Infested Citadel.
7. Cross into **Ashlands** on a Drakkar, mine Flametal, build a Stone Portal to stop hauling ore by boat, and take on [Fader](/bosses/fader) — the current endgame.

## What's next

Ashlands is the last released biome as of today. Valheim 1.0 adds an eighth biome, the Deep North, on **September 9, 2026** — see the [1.0 release hub](/valheim-1-0) and [Deep North guide](/deep-north) for what's confirmed so far about what comes after Fader.
