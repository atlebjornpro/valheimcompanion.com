Amber is mined from **red-orange veins inside the Revelwood Shroud**. A reliable cluster lies in the deep Shroud cavity between the **Abandoned Hunter Camp** and **Hidden Hollow Tomb**, north of the Revelwood Ancient Spire.

## At a glance

| Detail | Answer |
|---|---|
| Region | Revelwood |
| Resource level | 13 |
| Deposit appearance | Red-orange veins in Shrouded rock |
| Reliable landmarks | Abandoned Hunter Camp and Hidden Hollow Tomb |
| Main progression use | Strengthening the Flame to level 4 |

## Best verified Amber location

Unlock the **Revelwood Ancient Spire** first. From the spire, travel north toward the deep Shroud cavity between Abandoned Hunter Camp and Hidden Hollow Tomb. Several Amber deposits are embedded in the Shrouded rock there.

Because the deposits sit in the Shroud, identify a safe exit before mining. Do not spend the full timer on the first vein; leave enough time to climb or grapple back out.

## How to collect Amber efficiently

1. Bring a repaired pickaxe and empty several inventory slots.
2. Use the Revelwood Ancient Spire as your travel point.
3. Enter the Shroud cavity north of the spire and look for red-orange veins.
4. Mine the visible face, then check behind it because ore deposits extend into voxel terrain.
5. Leave before the Shroud timer becomes critical and return after resetting it.

## What Amber is used for

Amber is required to strengthen the Flame to **level 4**. It also appears in mage, healer, guardian, and other equipment recipes, plus spells such as Fireball II and Eternal Fireball.

Use the [Flame Planner](/tools/flame-planner) to see the complete upgrade bundle before making the trip.
