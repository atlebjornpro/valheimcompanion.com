Copper Ore is first found in **Revelwood**, where it appears as mineable copper-colored veins. Revelwood is a level 10-15 region that requires **Flame level 3** to access safely through its deadly Shroud passage.

## At a glance

| Detail | Answer |
|---|---|
| Region | Revelwood |
| Resource level | 13 |
| Required regional progression | Flame level 3 |
| Processing station | Smelter or Blast Furnace |
| Main use | Copper Bars and Bronze progression |

## How to find Copper Ore

Explore mine and rock faces throughout Revelwood and watch for copper-colored veins. The region contains two named mines, **Mark of Sameth** and **Glimmer Rock Mine**, but deposits also occur elsewhere in Revelwood; do not restrict the search to a single landmark.

Unlock the Revelwood Ancient Spire to shorten repeat trips. Bring a repaired pickaxe and mine beyond the visible surface, as the ore body can continue into the terrain.

## Unlock the Smelter

Complete the Blacksmith quest **Crucible Needed For A Smelter** and recover the Crucible from Mark of Sameth. The Crucible unlocks the Smelter needed for the normal Copper Bar recipe.

## Copper Bar recipes

| Station | Ingredients | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Smelter | 20 Copper Ore + 20 Charcoal | 10 Copper Bars |
| Blast Furnace | 30 Copper Ore + 30 Coal | 20 Copper Bars |

Copper Bars are used for tools, equipment, crafting stations, and Bronze Bars. The Smelter Bronze recipe combines **7 Copper Bars, 3 Tin Bars, and 10 Charcoal** for 10 Bronze Bars.
