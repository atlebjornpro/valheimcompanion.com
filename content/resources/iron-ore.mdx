Iron Ore is mined from veins in **Shrouded mines and cave passages in the Kindlewastes**. The clearest guided route is the Blacksmith's **Hearts Of Iron** quest, which marks **Ridgeback Mine** on the map.

## At a glance

| Detail | Answer |
|---|---|
| Region | Kindlewastes |
| Resource level | 25 |
| Guided location | Ridgeback Mine |
| Quest | Hearts Of Iron |
| Quest requirements | Level 19 and Shroud Passage level 5 |
| Main use | Iron Bars and Steel Bars |

## Best verified Iron Ore route

Progress the Blacksmith's quests until **Hearts Of Iron** becomes available. The quest requires character level 19 and Shroud Passage level 5, then places a marker on Ridgeback Mine.

Prepare for a late-game Shroud expedition:

1. Repair your pickaxe and equipment.
2. Bring healing and Shroud-time consumables.
3. Clear or avoid enemies before committing to a long mining animation.
4. Mine beyond the exposed surface of each vein.
5. Mark the mine for later return trips.

## What Iron Ore is used for

Iron Ore is processed into Iron Bars and contributes to Steel Bar production. Both metals feed late-game tools, weapons, armor, crafting stations, and construction recipes.
