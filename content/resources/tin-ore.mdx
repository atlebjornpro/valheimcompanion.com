Tin Ore is mined from veins in **Shrouded mines and cave passages in the Nomad Highlands**. The region is intended for roughly levels 15-20 and requires **Flame level 4**.

## At a glance

| Detail | Answer |
|---|---|
| Region | Nomad Highlands |
| Resource level | 18 |
| Deposit area | Shrouded mines and cave passages |
| Processing station | Smelter or Blast Furnace |
| Main use | Tin Bars for Bronze Bars |

## How to find Tin Ore

Unlock the Nomad Highlands Ancient Spire, then search the region's Shrouded mines and cave passages. **Mining Rift** and **Smothering Pit** are named mines in the region, but verify the vein inside each location rather than assuming every mine contains Tin.

Tin veins sit in the Shroud, so prioritize Shroud time over clearing every enemy. Mark a productive deposit on your map and return with a repaired pickaxe.

## Tin Bar recipes

| Station | Ingredients | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Smelter | 15 Tin Ore + 5 Charcoal + 2 Wood Acid | 3 Tin Bars |
| Blast Furnace | 20 Tin Ore + 6 Coal + 4 Wood Acid | 6 Tin Bars |

The Smelter Bronze recipe uses **7 Copper Bars, 3 Tin Bars, and 10 Charcoal** to produce 10 Bronze Bars. Gather the Wood Acid and fuel before mining so the ore does not sit unused at your base.
