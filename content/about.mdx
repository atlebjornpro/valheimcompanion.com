---
title: About Valheim Companion
description: The scope, independence, and purpose of Valheim Companion.
updated: 2026-08-04
---

Valheim Companion is an independent community project focused on practical, source-backed guidance for Valheim 1.0, the Deep North, dedicated servers, crossplay, world migration, backups, and hosting decisions.

The project does not attempt to replace broad Valheim wikis or seed-map services. It organizes a smaller set of time-sensitive decisions and operational procedures.

Valheim Companion is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Iron Gate AB or Coffee Stain Publishing. Valheim and related names belong to their respective owners.
