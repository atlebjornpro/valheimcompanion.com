Enshrouded Companion is an independent community guide built to make Embervale easier to understand and plan around.

The site focuses on tasks that benefit from a concise explanation, a searchable reference, or an interactive tool:

- Progression and regional preparation.
- Flame upgrades and skill-point planning.
- Resources, survivors, crafting, food, and base systems.
- Persistent co-op worlds and dedicated-server administration.
- Checklists that save progress locally without requiring an account.

## Why it was created

Broad wikis are excellent reference libraries, but players often need a direct answer or a planning tool rather than another long index. Enshrouded Companion reorganizes verified information around practical decisions: what to collect next, where progression leads, how to prepare a server, and which requirements are still missing.

The project builds on experience from creating Palworld Companion while keeping Enshrouded's information, design, and recommendations separate.

## Independence

Enshrouded Companion is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Keen Games. Enshrouded names and related game material belong to their respective owners.

The site is supported partly through clearly disclosed affiliate links. Affiliate availability does not determine factual conclusions or recommendation order. See the [Privacy and Affiliate Disclosure](/privacy) and [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy).

## Corrections and feedback

Game information changes and mistakes are possible. If you find an outdated requirement, incorrect location, broken tool, or unclear explanation, use the [contact page](/contact) to report it with the game version and supporting evidence.
