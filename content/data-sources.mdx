Valheim Companion prioritizes sources in this order:

1. Official Iron Gate and Valheim announcements.
2. Official Valheim support documentation and the dedicated-server manual distributed with the server application.
3. Current live-game or server behavior reproduced against a recorded version.
4. Platform-owner documentation for Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, or hosting infrastructure.

Community reports can identify questions but are not treated as confirmed facts without independent verification. Provider marketing is attributed to the provider and rechecked before comparison pages are published.

## World and biome guides

Iron Gate does not publish a formal encyclopedia of biomes, creatures, or bosses. For the [world guide](/world) and its biome pages, which describe the released, stable game rather than unreleased or preview content, this site cross-references at least two independent, established community wiki or guide sources per page and only publishes a claim where those sources agree. Exact numeric values (damage, HP, drop percentages) are omitted rather than reported when sources disagree or a figure could only be confirmed once — those pages describe mechanics qualitatively instead. Each page's review date reflects when it was last checked against current sources; a patch that changes a biome's enemies, resources, or a boss fight will be reflected at the next review rather than assumed unchanged.

Primary official entry points: [Valheim news](https://www.valheimgame.com/news/), [server guide](https://valheim.com/support/a-guide-to-dedicated-servers/), and [crossplay FAQ](https://valheim.com/support/crossplay-faq/).
