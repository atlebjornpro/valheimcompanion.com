---
title: Editorial Policy
description: How Valheim Companion separates confirmed facts, testing, interpretation, and commercial content.
updated: 2026-08-04
---

Pages identify confirmed facts, pending questions, measured observations, and editorial recommendations separately. Time-sensitive mechanics are not published from preview imagery alone.

Each substantive guide should record a review date and cite its primary sources. Corrections should update both the claim and its review status. When official documentation and observed behavior disagree, the difference should be described rather than silently resolved.

Commercial relationships do not establish recommendation order. Any affiliate link or sponsored relationship will be disclosed near the relevant content and in the privacy policy.
