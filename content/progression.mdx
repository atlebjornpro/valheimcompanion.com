Enshrouded progression combines character levels with Flame strength, rescued survivors, crafting stations, regional resources, and skill points earned across the world.

## Recommended route

1. Begin with the spoiler-light [Getting Started guide](/getting-started).
2. Rescue and place the [Survivors](/progression/survivors) who unlock essential crafting.
3. Use the [Flame Upgrade Planner](/tools/flame-planner) to prepare regional materials and boss trophies.
4. Check [Regions of Embervale](/world/regions) before moving into a new biome.
5. Track major milestones with the [Adventure Checklist](/checklist).

## Character planning

The [Skill Point Calculator](/tools/skill-points) combines points from levels, Shroud Roots, and Elixir Wells. [Build Foundations](/guides/builds) explains how equipment, food, and connected skill-tree routes work together.
