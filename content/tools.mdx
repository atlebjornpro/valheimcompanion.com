These browser-based tools turn common Enshrouded planning tasks into quick lookups and checklists. Progress saved by a tool remains in your browser unless the page states otherwise.

## Available tools

- [Flame Upgrade Planner](/tools/flame-planner) — review every current Flame upgrade and track the required materials.
- [Skill Point Calculator](/tools/skill-points) — combine points from levels, Shroud Roots, and Elixir Wells.
- [Resource Finder](/tools/resources) — search important materials by region, source, and crafting use.
- [Rested Calculator](/tools/rested) — estimate level-scaled Rested benefits and plan a Comfort target.
- [Adventure Checklist](/checklist) — track major survivors, regions, crafting unlocks, and progression milestones.

The tools are decision helpers rather than replacements for the in-game interface. Values are reviewed against the current version listed on the [update status page](/updates/current).
