## Frequently asked questions

### Which Valheim items are included here?

This first collection covers 26 items introduced with Valheim 1.0, centered on Deep North progression. Earlier-biome items will be added in later batches.

### Where should I start?

Build the Fader-to-[Ember Charge](/items/ember-charge) supply chain, make [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold), then clear [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) for the workshop and key route.
