Enshrouded **leaves Steam Early Access on October 15, 2026**. Keen Games announced the date on June 25 and said that more information about improvements, optimization, quality-of-life changes, and new content will be shared before release.

This page separates confirmed information from open questions. It will be updated as Keen Games publishes concrete details.

## Release at a glance

| Item | Confirmed status |
|---|---|
| Steam 1.0 release date | October 15, 2026 |
| Current live version reviewed here | v0.9.1.2 |
| Current live-version review date | July 27, 2026 |
| Console release date | No exact date stated in the linked announcement |
| Existing-save compatibility | Not confirmed in the linked announcement |
| Dedicated-server migration requirements | Not confirmed in the linked announcement |

## What Keen Games has confirmed

- Enshrouded will leave Early Access on Steam on October 15, 2026.
- More information is planned about improvements and optimization.
- More quality-of-life and new-content details will be announced ahead of launch.

The announcement video may show previews of launch content, but this page will not convert visual hints into confirmed mechanics until Keen Games describes them explicitly.

## What remains open

The release-date announcement does not by itself confirm:

- Whether existing worlds or characters will require migration.
- Whether progression values, Flame requirements, recipes, or skill totals will change.
- Which server configuration properties will be added or removed.
- A precise console launch schedule.
- The complete list of new regions, enemies, equipment, or quests.

Treat unsourced feature lists and exact console dates as speculation until an official announcement provides them.

## Companion guide and tool status

The current guides and calculators target **v0.9.1.2**. The highest-risk areas after a major update are:

1. Flame upgrade materials and benefits.
2. Skill-point totals and skill-tree routes.
3. Resource locations and regional progression.
4. Recipes, workstations, food, and equipment.
5. Dedicated-server settings, roles, and requirements.

At launch, these pages will retain their visible version labels until their data has been checked. A page will not be marked 1.0-ready merely because it still loads.
