The **Revelwood** is a dense level 10-15 forest region that requires **Flame level 3**. Its major progression resources are Copper Ore, Amber, Hardwood, and Clay, with Pikemead's Reach and the Blackmire adding more difficult routes.

## Priority route

1. Unlock the Revelwood Ancient Spire.
2. Establish a small forward Flame Altar outside dangerous Shroud.
3. Gather [Copper Ore](/resources/copper-ore) and complete the Blacksmith's Crucible quest.
4. Start Copper Bar and Bronze preparation.
5. Gather [Amber](/resources/amber) for Flame level 4.
6. Continue main survivor workstation quests.
7. Explore Pikemead's Reach only after upgrading current-tier equipment.
8. Treat Blackmire as a harder sub-region rather than ordinary Revelwood forest.

## Signature resources

| Resource | Main purpose |
|---|---|
| [Copper Ore](/resources/copper-ore) | Copper Bars, tools, stations, and Bronze |
| [Amber](/resources/amber) | Flame level 4, spells, and equipment |
| Hardwood | Carpenter progression and equipment |
| Lump of Clay | Bricks, Kiln recipes, and construction |
| Flax | Linen and textile progression |

## Hazards

Expect stronger Fell, Scavengers, aggressive wildlife, poisonous plants, and vertical terrain. The eastern Shrouded areas around Pikemead's Reach are significantly more dangerous than the southern approach.

## Ready for Nomad Highlands?

Prepare Flame level 4, Bronze-tier production, upgraded equipment, Shroud consumables, and a good ranged answer. Tin processing also needs Wood Acid and fuel, so stock those before the next ore run.
