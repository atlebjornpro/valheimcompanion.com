**Ashlands is Valheim's seventh biome** and the biome before Deep North — a lava-scarred endgame zone built around fire hazards and top-tier gear. This guide covers what lives here, how to work Flametal, and how to beat its boss, Fader. See [how this site sources world guides](/data-sources#world-and-biome-guides).

> **Update — September 2026**: Deep North is now playable. The 1.0 notes also adjust Ashlands spawns and increase Ashlands gear durability. See [patch notes](/patch-notes).

## Where Ashlands fits

Ashlands follows Mistlands and requires defeating The Queen to unlock the Drakkar, the only ship durable enough to survive the boiling ocean surrounding the Ashlands landmass. Fire-resistant, Flametal-tier gear is needed to progress once you land.

## Creatures

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Charred Warrior | Hostile; common ground melee fighter. |

| Charred Warlock | Hostile; the most dangerous common Charred type — fireballs and summons Twitchers. |
| Morgen | Hostile; fast melee ambush predator that bursts from the ground. |
| Fallen Valkyrie | Hostile; flying, alternates melee swoops with fire/poison ranged attacks. |
| Lava Blob | Hostile; explodes when approached for heavy blunt and fire damage. |

Charred-type enemies resist pierce damage and are immune to poison; spirit, frost, and lightning are especially effective against them.

## Boss: Fader

Fader is fought in a coliseum-like arena.

- **Summon:** place 3 Bells around the coliseum; craft each from 3 Bell Fragments found in Charred Fortresses (9 fragments total).
- **Finding the arena:** a Vegvísir found in a Charred Fortress's central tower marks its location.
- **Drops:** Fader Relics and a Fader Trophy (guaranteed).
- **Why it matters:** mounting the trophy grants a Forsaken Power with combat and movement bonuses. Fader precedes [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north). The 1.0 notes list updated Fader Relic functionality without describing the full unlock chain.

## Building and survival tips

- Cinder rain falls persistently across the biome, damaging structures and able to ignite wood — build with fireproof Grausten stone instead.
- A Shield Generator creates a protective dome blocking cinder rain and projectile damage; it's fueled by bone materials.
- Boiling water and lava immersion build up heat over time before dealing serious damage — Flametal armor's heat resistance helps.
- Crossing lava fields may require Basalt Bombs to create temporary platforms.
- Stick to coastal routes when possible, avoid nighttime exploration, and spawnproof travel routes with campfires — Ashlands has a high density of hostile spawns.

## Points of interest

Charred Fortresses (walled structures marked by a green light beam, the main source of Bell Fragments and Flametal, guarded by Warlocks and turret-like Skugg), Putrid Holes (smaller dungeons holding Molten Cores), lava fields, and Dvergr camps.
