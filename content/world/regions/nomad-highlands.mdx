The **Nomad Highlands** is a level 15-20 region of limestone bluffs and deep Shrouded lowlands. It requires **Flame level 4** and introduces Tin Ore, a key material for Bronze progression.

## Priority route

1. Unlock the Nomad Highlands Ancient Spire.
2. Mark safe routes around Umber Hollow and other deep Shroud.
3. Mine [Tin Ore](/resources/tin-ore) in Shrouded mines and cave passages.
4. Process Tin Bars and combine them with Copper Bars for Bronze.
5. Gather Limestone and Fossilized Bone while exploring.
6. Clear reachable Roots and Wells before leaving the region.
7. Upgrade weapons and armor before pushing toward Kindlewastes.

## Signature resources

| Resource | Main purpose |
|---|---|
| [Tin Ore](/resources/tin-ore) | Tin Bars and Bronze Bars |
| Limestone | Building and advanced crafting |
| Fossilized Bone | Flame and crafting progression |
| Copper Ore | Additional Copper/Bronze supply |
| Sulfur | Alchemy, explosives, and ammunition |

The region's named mines include Mining Rift and Smothering Pit. Tin can occur in Nomad Highlands Shrouded mines and cave passages, but verify the vein at each location rather than assuming every mine contains it.

## Smelting preparation

The standard Smelter recipe produces 3 Tin Bars from **15 Tin Ore, 5 Charcoal, and 2 Wood Acid**. Bring back enough ore for complete batches and keep Copper Bars available for Bronze production.

## Ready for Kindlewastes?

Reach Flame level 5, upgrade Bronze-tier equipment, carry strong healing and Shroud consumables, and leave inventory space for Iron Ore. The Blacksmith's **Hearts Of Iron** quest later marks Ridgeback Mine.
