The **Deep North** is the biome added in Valheim 1.0. For gear families and world migration, use the [Deep North guide](/deep-north).

## Where Deep North fits

Deep North follows [Ashlands](/world/regions/ashlands) in this site's progression reference. Iron Gate lists updated functionality for the Fader Relic, but does not explain the full progression gate in the patch notes. See the [roadmap](/progression/roadmap).

## Creatures

| Official release listing | Names |
|---|---|
| Enemies | Barka, Elaking, Eyeless One, Fallen Warrior, Gammeltroll, Hexen, Krigen |
| Pulp enemies | Hexahedric Pulp, Shapeless Pulp, Tiny Pulp |
| Moose | Tameable and rideable; Moose Calf also listed |
| Other creatures | Seal, Baby Seal, Writhan |
| Boss | Kall Fimbulbringer |

These are update-wide names, not verified Deep North spawn distributions. The notes also introduce alternate earlier biomes. No health or resistance table is published in the cited announcement.

## Boss: Kall Fimbulbringer

The notes identify **[Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer)** as the new boss. Summoning quantities, arena access, attacks, drops, and Forsaken Power details are not published in the cited notes as of September 17, 2026. The boss page records those gaps individually.

## Building and survival tips

- Clear deep snow with the Snow Shovel before building.
- Watch footing on slippery ice and frozen seas. No safe ice-load or cracking threshold is documented in the cited sources.
- Check the installed hotfix if Deep North heavy armor cannot be repaired; repairability was fixed in 1.0.10.
- Back up an established world before migration. New biome generation requires unexplored terrain according to the FAQ.

## Points of interest

Iron Gate names **Winding Tunnels** and **Mörkhalla** as dungeons. Locations include an abandoned village, stave hall, Ice Pond, memorial site, and Forge of Potential. The notes do not provide a location-by-biome distribution or complete loot tables.
