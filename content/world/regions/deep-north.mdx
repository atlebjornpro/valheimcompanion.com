**Deep North is the final biome in Valheim's boss progression.** This reference covers its creatures, material uses, dungeons, and survival preparation. For the workshop sequence and an expedition checklist, follow the [Deep North guide](/deep-north).

Details below are cross-checked against community guides where Iron Gate's notes do not supply the mechanics.

## Where Deep North fits

Deep North follows [Ashlands](/world/regions/ashlands) and [Fader](/bosses/fader). His Relic makes the Eternal Pyre that supplies Embers; the [Ember Charge](/items/ember-charge) guide follows that bridge through [Petrified Tissue](/items/petrified-tissue) to [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold). Bring that supply chain with you rather than expecting every ingredient to come from northern dungeons.

| Stage | Main destination |
|---|---|
| Previous | [Ashlands and Fader](/world/regions/ashlands) |
| Current | Deep North: surface materials → [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) → [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) |
| Finale | [Jotun Invasions](/world/locations/jotun-invasion) → [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) → [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) |

See the [progression roadmap](/progression/roadmap) for the earlier biomes.

## Creatures

| Creature | Encounter and useful drops |
|---|---|
| Seal | Coastal wildlife; Seal Pelt and Seal Blubber |
| Moose | Tameable, rideable animal; Moose Meat, Hide, and Sinew |
| Gammeltroll | Large surface troll; its petrified remains yield Petrified Tissue when broken with an Ember Charge |
| Barka | Frozen tree-like enemy; Frozen Branch |
| Elaking | Winding Tunnels enemy; [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key) mould and [Elaking Hair Bundle](/items/elaking-hair-bundle) |
| Eyeless One | Winding Tunnels enemy; [Long Claws](/items/long-claws) |
| Krigen | Mörkhalla enemy; Leather Straps, [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal), and Vanguard armor moulds |
| Hexen | Mörkhalla enemy; [Nornathread](/items/nornathread) and Caller armor moulds |
| Fallen Warrior | [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar) encounter; can drop [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) and [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence) |

These are the material drops most relevant to progression, not exhaustive loot tables. The official update also names several Pulp variants and Writhan; they are not assigned unverified drop tables here.

## Resources

| Resource | Acquisition and use |
|---|---|
| [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) | Smelt Petrified Tissue in a Blast Furnace; used in equipment and key casts |
| [Frostcore](/items/frostcore) | Gather in Winding Tunnels; needed to construct the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry |
| [Ice](/items/ice) / [Liquid Frost](/items/liquid-frost) | The Frigid Kiln converts 5 Ice to 1 Liquid Frost; the latter fuels the Frost Foundry |
| Frostfire Essence / Thunderblood Essence | Fallen Warrior rewards from Ancient Altars; used for the respective weapon upgrades |
| Memorial Coal | Collected from Krigen in Mörkhalla; offered at Ancient Altars to summon Fallen Warriors |
| Nornathread | Hexen drop; used in Caller armor and magical equipment |
| Seal Pelt / Embers | Combined into Ember Charges for breaking petrified Gammeltroll remains; Embers come from the Eternal Pyre |
| Frozen Branch | Barka drop; used in equipment such as the Nord Greatsword and Spirit Caller |
| Lingonberries | Harvestable food ingredient |
| Timberwood, Kale, Oats, Poteitr | Plantable resources listed in the 1.0 release |

The **Black Forge makes casts** from moulds and recipe materials. The **Frost Foundry turns those casts into finished equipment or keys**, using Liquid Frost from the **Frigid Kiln**. Smith's Aprons, Standing Loom, and Smoker extend the Black Forge, Galdr Table, and Cauldron respectively.

## Boss: Kall Fimbulbringer

- **Summon:** offer [3 Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood) in the Strange Bowl at the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) to enter **The Prison**.
- **Blood route:** unlock Mörkhalla with Intricate Keys, break its Malicious Ice, then complete the resulting Jotun Invasion for one Blood. Repeat for three.
- **Fight:** three phases, including a middle gauntlet of earlier Forsaken Aspects while Kall is immune.
- **Rewards:** Sacrificial Blood and Crown Jewel; **no Forsaken Power**.

Use the [full boss guide](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) for attacks, equipment, the disputed health totals, and the ending.

## Building and survival tips

- Clear deep snow with a **Snow Shovel** before laying out an outpost.
- Watch slippery ice and frozen water. Keep a retreat route and stamina reserve when exploring the shoreline.
- Establish storage and a protected portal before long dungeon trips; keep a supply of Liquid Frost at your workshop so returning with a mould can lead straight to an upgrade.
- Check [hotfix 1.0.10](/patch-notes/v1-0-10) if Deep North heavy armor cannot be repaired.
- For older saves, new biome generation requires unexplored terrain. Read the [world migration guidance](/servers/existing-world-vs-new-world) before heading north.

## Points of interest

**[Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels)**, reached beneath [abandoned villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village), provide Frostcores and key moulds. They are the preparation dungeon for your workshop and the next dungeon tier.

**[Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla)** requires an Intricate Key. Its enemies supply armor materials and moulds; its Vegvisir marks the Aesir Passage. Destroying the Malicious Ice at the bottom starts a Jotun Invasion elsewhere in the world. Breaking the invasion core yields Malicious Blood for the boss offering.

**[Ancient Altars](/world/locations/ancient-altar)** turn Memorial Coal into Fallen Warrior encounters and a chance at weapon essences. **[Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage)** is the final destination once your three Blood and boss loadout are ready. The [Forge of Potential](/world/locations/forge-of-potential) is an optional equipment station rather than a required Kall step.
