**Plains is Valheim's fifth biome**, and widely considered the most dangerous relative to the gear tier available when players first arrive. This guide covers what lives here, how to farm Black Metal, and how to beat the fifth boss, Yagluth. See [how this site sources world guides](/data-sources#world-and-biome-guides).

## Where Plains fits

Plains follows Mountain. There's no key or item gate stopping entry, but its enemies hit far above the biome's position in the tech tree — silver-tier gear at minimum is recommended. Farming Black Metal here and defeating Yagluth prepares players for [Mistlands](/world/regions/mistlands), the next biome.

## Creatures

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Deathsquito | Hostile; small and fast, but can kill even well-armored players in a single hit. Block or snipe before it closes in. |
| Lox | Passive until provoked; large and slow, tameable with Cloudberries, rideable once tamed. |
| Fuling | Hostile; the biome's main enemy, found alone or in villages. |
| Fuling Berserker | Hostile; tougher village elite with a heavy smash attack — dodge rather than block. |
| Fuling Shaman | Hostile; casts protective shields on nearby Fulings — priority kill. |
| Growth | Hostile; tar creature guarding Tar Pits, spits slowing/poisoning tar. Weak to blunt and fire. |

## Boss: Yagluth

Yagluth's altar is a distinctive set of massive hand-shaped standing stones within the Plains.

- **Summon:** place **5 Fuling Totems** on the altar. Totems drop from Fuling Berserkers and appear in village chests.
- **Finding the altar:** a Vegvísir at nearby rune circles marks its location.
- **Drops:** a Yagluth Trophy and Torn Spirits.
- **Why it matters:** mounting the trophy grants temporary resistance to fire, frost, and lightning damage. Defeating Yagluth is the conventional readiness milestone for Mistlands, and unlocks new events and trader offerings.

## Building and survival tips

- Keep a shield raised at all times — Deathsquitos and regular Fulings both punish players caught without a block ready.
- Approach Fuling villages with stealth, sniping Shamans and Berserkers before engaging the main group.
- Build small fortified forward bases rather than full settlements, connected by portal for fast retreat.
- Deathsquitos can spawn on beaches and will chase far out over open water — stay alert while sailing near Plains coastlines.

## Points of interest

Fuling Villages (built from stone, bone, and hide, holding Barley, Flax, Black Metal Scrap, and Totems), Tar Pits guarded by Growths, scattered Fuling outposts, and Yagluth's hand-shaped Forsaken Altar.
