**Meadows is Valheim's starting biome** — every new world begins here, and it stays the lowest-risk place to build a first base. This guide covers what actually lives here, what to gather, and how to beat the first boss, Eikthyr. See [how this site sources world guides](/data-sources#world-and-biome-guides).

## Where Meadows fits

Meadows opens the game's first progression tier alongside Black Forest. Once Eikthyr is down and an Antler Pickaxe is in hand, [Black Forest](/world/regions/black-forest) is next.

## Creatures

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Boar | Hostile; low threat once armed. Tameable with food. |
| Neck | Hostile; the easiest early kill, common near water. |
| Greyling | Hostile; low damage, drops Resin. |
| Deer | Passive; flees on sight, main source of Deer Hide and Deer Trophies. |
| Gull (Seagull) | Passive; coastal, minor nuisance. |
| Draugr | Hostile, but only inside Draugr Villages — not a roaming Meadows spawn. |

No creature here is exclusive to Meadows; all of them, aside from the Draugr Village exception, also appear at low tier in other early biomes.

## Resources

| Resource | Source |
|---|---|
| Wood, Fine Wood | Birch, Beech, and Oak trees |
| Stone, Flint | Ground and shorelines |
| Resin | Greylings, some trees |
| Leather Scraps, Deer Hide, Raw Meat, Feathers | Animal drops |
| Dandelion, Mushroom, Raspberry | Foraged plants |

No mineable ore is found in Meadows — that starts once a pickaxe is available, in Black Forest and coastal areas nearby.

## Boss: Eikthyr

Eikthyr is the first of Valheim's bosses, fought at a Sacrificial Stones altar located somewhere in Meadows.

- **Summon:** place **2 Deer Trophies** on the altar.
- **Finding the altar:** a Vegvisir rune stone (found in burial chambers) points to the nearest one if it isn't already discovered.
- **Drops:** 1 Eikthyr Trophy and 3 Hard Antlers.
- **Why it matters:** the Hard Antlers craft the Antler Pickaxe — the game's first pickaxe, and the gate to mining ore and the whole metal-tool tier. Mounting the trophy also grants a Forsaken Power that reduces stamina cost for running and jumping.

## Building and survival tips

- Meadows' flat, low-threat terrain makes it a good spot for a first permanent base — and since it's near the world center, it doubles well as a portal hub later.
- Draugr Villages are a real step up in difficulty from the rest of the biome; it's worth waiting for Bronze-tier gear before clearing one.
- Coastal stretches are convenient for early Flint and Stone, but Boars nearby can turn aggressive while foraging.
- No biome-specific weather hazard beyond the game's normal rain and wind — Meadows is the lowest-hazard biome overall.

## Points of interest

Burial Mounds (ship-shaped earthworks with a buried chest, need a pickaxe to open), Abandoned Houses, wooden Watchtowers, Standing Stone circles, and Draugr Villages. There are no true dungeons in Meadows.
