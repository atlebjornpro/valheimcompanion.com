The **Springlands** is Enshrouded's starting region, covering roughly levels 1-10 and Flame levels 1-2. Your main objective is to rescue the foundational craftspeople and establish the tool, food, leather, and metalworking chains needed for Revelwood.

## Priority route

1. Establish the first Flame Altar and Workbench.
2. Craft the Glider and Grappling Hook.
3. Rescue the [Blacksmith](/progression/survivors/blacksmith).
4. Rescue the [Hunter](/progression/survivors/hunter) and [Alchemist](/progression/survivors/alchemist).
5. Continue the quest chain for the Carpenter and Farmer.
6. Unlock the Springlands Ancient Spire.
7. Clear reachable Shroud Roots and Elixir Wells for skill points.
8. Gather the materials for Flame levels 2 and 3.

## Important resources

| Resource | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Flintstone | Early tools and crafting |
| [Salt](/resources/salt) | Dried Fur, Leather, food, and later reagents |
| Metal Scraps | Early metal tools and stations |
| Shroud Wood and Shroud Liquid | Early recipes and Flame progression |
| Clay | Appears toward the Revelwood transition |

## Mines and landmarks

Springlands mines include Small Flint Mine, Egerton Salt Mines, and Saline Quarry. The Hunter's **Salt Mine Location** quest is the easiest guided route to a large Salt deposit.

Do not rush every Shrouded valley on the first visit. Increase the Flame and return with a longer timer, better pickaxe, and more inventory space.

## Ready for Revelwood?

Move on when you have the five foundational craftspeople, reliable food and healing, current armor, a repaired pickaxe, Flame level 3, and at least one unlocked Ancient Spire.
