## What the landmark is for

Stave Halls are named Deep North structures and useful navigation points, but current official and corroborating sources do not assign them a unique required drop or boss-chain interaction. Record the location, clear any occupants, and inspect normal containers without assuming every hall hides a key mould.

Their practical value is shelter and orientation during surface scouting. A marked hall can anchor a route between the coast, an Abandoned Village, and a portal outpost. Check structural safety and nearby enemy spawns before using one as a temporary base.

If the goal is Kall progression, move on after a complete ordinary search. [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels), [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla), Jotun Invasions, and the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) provide the required chain. This page stays intentionally cautious until a distinct Stave Hall reward is documented by reliable sources.
