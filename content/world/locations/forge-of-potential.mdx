## How to reach it


1. Explore the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) for the forge structure.

3. Read the in-game result and cost before confirming an attempt.

- A late-game station for special upgrade attempts.
- A reason to retain spare Deep North equipment and materials.

## Using the forge without blocking progression

The Forge of Potential is an optional Deep North station for special equipment upgrade attempts. Its interface tells you which current items it accepts and what the attempt requires. Read that live panel before committing materials, since the official 1.0 notes name the system without publishing a permanent success table.

Keep boss-route resources in separate storage. Three [Intricate Keys](/items/intricate-key), station construction, food, and repaired armor have priority over experimental upgrades. Do not feed the forge the only copy of equipment you need for [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) or Kall.

Mark the location and connect it to a portal before bringing valuables. If an item is rejected, that means the current version or item is unsupported; repeated interaction will not create an unlisted recipe. Treat the station as an endgame material sink after the mandatory progression chain is secure.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Forge of Potential mandatory?

No. The Kall route uses Winding Tunnels, Mörkhalla, three invasions, and the Aesir Passage.

### What success rate should I plan around?

Use the live station interface. Iron Gate has not published a stable universal percentage table, so this guide does not invent one.
