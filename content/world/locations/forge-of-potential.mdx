**The Forge of Potential is a naturally generated Mountain location, not a Deep North station.** It lets you risk a supported weapon, tool, or armor piece for one extra quality level. The attempt has a **65% chance to succeed** and a **35% chance to destroy the item**.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Biome | [Mountain](/world/regions/mountain) |
| Map behavior | A marker appears when you approach the location |
| Weapon or tool input | Matching-tier Battle Idol |
| Armor input | Matching-tier Protection Idol |
| Success | 65%: item gains one quality level |
| Failure | 35%: item is destroyed and some materials are returned |
| Required for Kall | No |

The probabilities and returned-material behavior above come from current game-data references. Iron Gate's release notes name the system but do not publish its complete numerical table.

## How to find the Forge of Potential

Search **Mountain biomes** rather than the Deep North. The forge is a generated structure positioned around mountainous terrain, and its icon is revealed when you travel close enough. A Wishbone is not required to reveal it.

Use a portal at a safe foothill or restored Mountain structure, then scout ridgelines in Frost-resistant gear. Once the map marker appears, label the portal with the forge location before transporting an item you cannot afford to strand.

## How an upgrade attempt works

1. Bring a supported item and an idol of the correct category and tier.
2. Place the item in the forge and check the previewed result.
3. Confirm only if losing that exact item will not block your progression.
4. On success, the item gains one quality level beyond its normal crafting path.
5. On failure, the item is destroyed. The forge returns **35% of the relevant crafting materials, rounded up**, based on the base item cost plus its most recent upgrade cost.

The station is therefore a gamble, not a conventional crafting bench. Two attempts do not guarantee a success, and a failed attempt does not return an intact lower-quality item.

## Battle Idols and Protection Idols

| Idol | Eligible equipment |
|---|---|
| Battle Idol | Weapons and tools |
| Protection Idol | Armor |

Idol tiers must match the equipment tier accepted by the forge. A rejected item or idol is usually a category or tier mismatch. Read the current interface before committing the item because later patches can change support.

## What is worth risking?

Start with a duplicate whose materials you can replace. An extra quality level is most useful on equipment you expect to keep using, but that same longevity makes the only copy a poor first gamble.

- Keep boss-route and recovery equipment out of the forge.
- Stock enough materials to rebuild the item after the partial refund.
- In multiplayer, agree who owns the item and who accepts the risk.
- Back up the world for disaster recovery, not as a routine way to reroll the result.

The forge is optional. It is not needed for [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla), the [Jotun Invasion](/world/locations/jotun-invasion), or [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Forge of Potential in the Deep North?

No. It generates in the Mountains. It arrived with Valheim 1.0 and can improve late-game gear, which likely caused it to be grouped incorrectly with Deep North content in early guides.

### Can the Forge of Potential destroy an item?

Yes. The current mechanic is a 65% upgrade chance and a 35% destruction chance. A failed attempt returns only part of the relevant materials.

### Is it required to beat Kall?

No. The forge is an optional equipment gamble and is separate from the required Deep North progression chain.
