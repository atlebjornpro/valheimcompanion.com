## How to reach it

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes name the content but do not publish the complete progression sequence.

1. Explore the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) for the forge structure.
2. Bring a supported item and the materials shown by the station interface.
3. Read the in-game result and cost before confirming an attempt.

## What you get from it

- A late-game station for special upgrade attempts.
- A reason to retain spare Deep North equipment and materials.
- An optional path alongside the main [Kall](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) progression.

## Tactics

- Treat the station as optional and keep your required progression gear separate.
- Do not rely on uncited success percentages; Iron Gate has not published them.
- Read the current interface because patches can change supported items.
- Back up a world before experimenting if losing materials would block progression.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you find Forge of Potential in Valheim?

Explore the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) for the forge structure. Then follow the remaining steps above; every required item and preceding location is linked.

### What do you get from Forge of Potential?

Its main verified reward or purpose is attempting special equipment upgrades. Exact drop quantities are omitted unless multiple sources agree.

### Is Forge of Potential required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

No. It is useful or optional Deep North content; the direct boss route runs through [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels), [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla), Jotun Invasions, and the Aesir Passage.
