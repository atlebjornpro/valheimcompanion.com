**Winding Tunnels is a Deep North dungeon tied to Frostcores, Intricate Key moulds, Elaking Hair, and Long Claws.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it


1. Search [Abandoned Villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village) in the Deep North.
2. Check the largest building and the ground beneath it for the dungeon entrance.
3. Mark the entrance and establish a portal before starting a full clear.

## What you get from it

- [Frostcores](/items/frostcore) for the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry.
- A mould used to make the [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key).
- [Elaking Hair Bundles](/items/elaking-hair-bundle) and [Long Claws](/items/long-claws) from dungeon enemies.

## Tactics

- Bring rested food, healing, and inventory space for cores and moulds.
- Fight from cleared rooms rather than pulling enemies into unexplored passages.
- Mark branches so a retreat does not become another search.
- Do not leave before confirming whether the key mould dropped.

## Dungeon route and progression checklist

Winding Tunnels are large multi-room dungeons beneath [Abandoned Villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village). Expect Elaking, Draugr variants, skeleton variants, and Eyeless Ones. Elaking supply [Elaking Hair Bundles](/items/elaking-hair-bundle), while Eyeless Ones supply [Long Claws](/items/long-claws). Pull each group into a cleared room and mark branches so a retreat remains readable.

[Frostcores](/items/frostcore) appear embedded through walls, floors, and dungeon features rather than only as a final chest reward. The first pickup reveals the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry; keep collecting until you have 20 for both stations. Key moulds can appear in dungeon loot, and each mould is consumed when a cast is made. Search for three before leaving if the dungeon layout allows it.

Place a portal outside the entrance and bank rare materials between branches. Carry a hoe or signs for route marking, food, healing, and enough weapon durability for a long clear. The practical loot order is Frostcores first, three key moulds second, then equipment moulds and enemy materials. A single mould is only one-third of the [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) route.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many key moulds should I collect?

Aim for three. Each mould is consumed to make one cast, and three finished keys are required for the Mörkhalla loop.

### How many Frostcores should I leave with?

Collect at least 20 to build both the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry, then keep extras needed by later crafting.
