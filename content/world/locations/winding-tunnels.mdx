**Winding Tunnels is a Deep North dungeon tied to Frostcores, Intricate Key moulds, Elaking Hair, and Long Claws.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes name the content but do not publish the complete progression sequence.

1. Search [Abandoned Villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village) in the Deep North.
2. Check the largest building and the ground beneath it for the dungeon entrance.
3. Mark the entrance and establish a portal before starting a full clear.

## What you get from it

- [Frostcores](/items/frostcore) for the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry.
- A mould used to make the [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key).
- [Elaking Hair Bundles](/items/elaking-hair-bundle) and [Long Claws](/items/long-claws) from dungeon enemies.

## Tactics

- Bring rested food, healing, and inventory space for cores and moulds.
- Fight from cleared rooms rather than pulling enemies into unexplored passages.
- Mark branches so a retreat does not become another search.
- Do not leave before confirming whether the key mould dropped.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you find Winding Tunnels in Valheim?

Search [Abandoned Villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village) in the Deep North. Then follow the remaining steps above; every required item and preceding location is linked.

### What do you get from Winding Tunnels?

Its main verified reward or purpose is Frostcores, Intricate Key moulds, Elaking Hair, and Long Claws. Exact drop quantities are omitted unless multiple sources agree.

### Is Winding Tunnels required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

Yes. It is part of the chain that creates [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood) or opens the route to [Kall](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer).
