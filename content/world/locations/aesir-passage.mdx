**Aesir Passage is a Deep North poi tied to the Strange Bowl, the Prison arena, and Kall Fimbulbringer.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it


1. Clear [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) and use its Vegvisir to reveal the passage.
2. Complete three [Jotun Invasions](/world/locations/jotun-invasion) for three [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood).
3. Offer all three in the Strange Bowl.
4. Enter the Prison and fight [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer).

## What you get from it

- Access to the Prison boss arena.
- [Sacrificial Blood](/items/sacrificial-blood) and the [Crown Jewel](/items/crown-jewel) after defeating Kall.
- The final 1.0 progression step; Kall does not grant a Forsaken Power.

## Tactics

- Place and test a recovery portal before spending the blood.
- Bring fire and frost resistance plus healing and stamina recovery.
- Budget supplies for Kall's three-phase encounter.
- Do not confuse Malicious Blood with the post-fight Sacrificial Blood.

## Preparing the final approach

Use the Vegvisir found inside [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) to mark the Aesir Passage. The site contains the Strange Bowl and the route into the Prison arena. Bring **three [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood)**—one from each completed Jotun Invasion—and interact with the bowl to open the final encounter.

Set a tested recovery portal outside the commitment point, repair every equipped item, and bring food, healing, stamina recovery, and fire and frost protection appropriate to your build. Store spare gear at the portal rather than carrying the whole dungeon haul into the arena.

The Prison beyond the passage holds [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer). The boss rewards a [Crown Jewel](/items/crown-jewel) and [Sacrificial Blood](/items/sacrificial-blood), but no Forsaken Power. The blood offered at the bowl is consumed before the fight; it cannot be replaced by the similarly named post-fight drop.

Before entering, sleep for a full rested timer and test the portal in both directions. Leave building materials, spare food, and a backup weapon outside the arena. If the first attempt fails, this staging turns recovery into a short preparation loop instead of another voyage through the Deep North.

## Frequently asked questions

### What opens the Strange Bowl?

Offer three Malicious Blood, earned from three separate Jotun Invasion cores.

### What is beyond the Aesir Passage?

It leads to the Prison arena and Kall Fimbulbringer. Set a recovery portal and finish preparation before making the offering.
