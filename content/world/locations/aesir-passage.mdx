**Aesir Passage is a Deep North poi tied to the Strange Bowl, the Prison arena, and Kall Fimbulbringer.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes name the content but do not publish the complete progression sequence.

1. Clear [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) and use its Vegvisir to reveal the passage.
2. Complete three [Jotun Invasions](/world/locations/jotun-invasion) for three [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood).
3. Offer all three in the Strange Bowl.
4. Enter the Prison and fight [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer).

## What you get from it

- Access to the Prison boss arena.
- [Sacrificial Blood](/items/sacrificial-blood) and the [Crown Jewel](/items/crown-jewel) after defeating Kall.
- The final 1.0 progression step; Kall does not grant a Forsaken Power.

## Tactics

- Place and test a recovery portal before spending the blood.
- Bring fire and frost resistance plus healing and stamina recovery.
- Budget supplies for Kall's three-phase encounter.
- Do not confuse Malicious Blood with the post-fight Sacrificial Blood.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you find Aesir Passage in Valheim?

Clear [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) and use its Vegvisir to reveal the passage. Then follow the remaining steps above; every required item and preceding location is linked.

### What do you get from Aesir Passage?

Its main verified reward or purpose is the Strange Bowl, the Prison arena, and Kall Fimbulbringer. Exact drop quantities are omitted unless multiple sources agree.

### Is Aesir Passage required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

Yes. It is part of the chain that creates [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood) or opens the route to [Kall](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer).
