**Ancient Altar is a Deep North poi tied to Frostfire or Thunderblood Essence from Fallen Warriors.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

| Requires | 3 Memorial Coal per Fallen Warrior summon |

## How to reach it


1. Explore [Memorial Sites](/world/locations/memorial-site) in the Deep North.
2. Bring [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) collected from Krigen in [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla).
3. Offer the coal and defeat the summoned Fallen Warrior.

## What you get from it

- A chance at [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence).
- A chance at [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence).
- A repeatable combat route for elemental weapon upgrades.

## Tactics

- Clear nearby creatures before making the offering.
- Bring a complete combat loadout rather than treating the altar as a passive container.
- Keep room to dodge around the memorial terrain.
- Expect a chance at an essence, not a guaranteed choice of type.

## Offering loop and rewards

Ancient Altars appear at Memorial Sites and run an optional combat loop. Bring **three [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal)**, clear the surrounding terrain, and place the offering. A Fallen Warrior spawns immediately, so arrive rested with room to dodge rather than opening the interaction while other Deep North creatures are nearby.

The warrior can drop [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) or [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence). One essence plus 20 Bloodgold converts eligible Nord weapons to the matching elemental form; the essences also support specific casts. The reward type is chance-based, so repeat summons may be needed.

This route is optional for Kall. It improves equipment but does not produce Intricate Keys, Malicious Blood, or access to the Aesir Passage. Protect the Bloodgold reserved for the three mandatory keys before funding elemental conversions.

For repeat farming, keep coal and spare food in a chest by the portal rather than carrying every offering at once. Reset the arena after each warrior, repair if needed, and confirm that no roaming creature entered the site. This keeps a chance-based reward loop from turning into a pile of overlapping recovery fights.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much Memorial Coal does the altar require?

Three Memorial Coal summons one Fallen Warrior.

### Is the Ancient Altar required for Kall?

No. It is an optional route to Frostfire and Thunderblood weapon essences.
