**Ancient Altar is a Deep North poi tied to Frostfire or Thunderblood Essence from Fallen Warriors.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes name the content but do not publish the complete progression sequence.

1. Explore [Memorial Sites](/world/locations/memorial-site) in the Deep North.
2. Bring [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) collected from Krigen in [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla).
3. Offer the coal and defeat the summoned Fallen Warrior.

## What you get from it

- A chance at [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence).
- A chance at [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence).
- A repeatable combat route for elemental weapon upgrades.

## Tactics

- Clear nearby creatures before making the offering.
- Bring a complete combat loadout rather than treating the altar as a passive container.
- Keep room to dodge around the memorial terrain.
- Expect a chance at an essence, not a guaranteed choice of type.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you find Ancient Altar in Valheim?

Explore [Memorial Sites](/world/locations/memorial-site) in the Deep North. Then follow the remaining steps above; every required item and preceding location is linked.

### What do you get from Ancient Altar?

Its main verified reward or purpose is Frostfire or Thunderblood Essence from Fallen Warriors. Exact drop quantities are omitted unless multiple sources agree.

### Is Ancient Altar required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

No. It is useful or optional Deep North content; the direct boss route runs through [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels), [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla), Jotun Invasions, and the Aesir Passage.
