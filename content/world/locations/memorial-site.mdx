**Memorial Site is a Deep North poi tied to an Ancient Altar and Fallen Warrior encounter.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes name the content but do not publish the complete progression sequence.

1. Explore the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) and mark memorial structures.
2. Locate the [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar) within the site.
3. Return with [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) from [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla).

## What you get from it

- The altar interaction that summons a Fallen Warrior.
- A route to [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) and [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence).
- A repeatable optional upgrade activity separate from the Kall offering chain.

## Tactics

- Clear the whole site before spending coal.
- Prepare for a combat encounter immediately after the offering.
- Keep coal for the altar rather than treating it as ordinary fuel.
- Do not confuse this surface site with the Sacrificial Stones ending location.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you find Memorial Site in Valheim?

Explore the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) and mark memorial structures. Then follow the remaining steps above; every required item and preceding location is linked.

### What do you get from Memorial Site?

Its main verified reward or purpose is an Ancient Altar and Fallen Warrior encounter. Exact drop quantities are omitted unless multiple sources agree.

### Is Memorial Site required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

No. It is useful or optional Deep North content; the direct boss route runs through [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels), [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla), Jotun Invasions, and the Aesir Passage.
