**Memorial Site is a Deep North poi tied to an Ancient Altar and Fallen Warrior encounter.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it


1. Explore the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) and mark memorial structures.
2. Locate the [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar) within the site.
3. Return with [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) from [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla).

## What you get from it

- The altar interaction that summons a Fallen Warrior.
- A route to [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) and [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence).
- A repeatable optional upgrade activity separate from the Kall offering chain.

## Tactics

- Clear the whole site before spending coal.
- Prepare for a combat encounter immediately after the offering.
- Keep coal for the altar rather than treating it as ordinary fuel.
- Do not confuse this surface site with the Sacrificial Stones ending location.

## Preparing the encounter space

A Memorial Site is the surface landmark containing an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar). Clear roaming creatures, flatten your escape route mentally, and place a portal outside the immediate fight area before using the altar. The interaction summons a Fallen Warrior rather than opening a passive loot container.

Bring three [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) for each attempt. The summoned warrior can reward [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) or [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence), but the desired type is not guaranteed. Carry several offering sets only if you are ready for repeated fights.

The site is an optional equipment branch. It does not reveal the Aesir Passage or replace the Mörkhalla invasions required for Kall. Mark it for later if the key, foundry, or food supply is still incomplete.

Use a distinct map label such as “altar” rather than another generic memorial marker. When several sites are discovered, note which has a portal and a cleared combat space. That prevents a three-coal offering run from ending at an unprepared site during bad weather or at night.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I bring to a Memorial Site?

Bring three Memorial Coal per summon, a rested combat loadout, and portal materials for repeat attempts.

### Does the site advance the Kall route?

No. Its Fallen Warrior offers optional elemental essences rather than keys or Malicious Blood.
