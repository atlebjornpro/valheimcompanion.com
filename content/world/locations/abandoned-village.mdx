## How to reach it

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes name the content but do not publish the complete progression sequence.

1. Scout visible structures in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
2. Search the village methodically and identify its largest house.
3. Check beneath that building for a [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) entrance.

## What you get from it

- A route into the first Deep North dungeon.
- Potential shelter and salvage during northern expeditions.
- Access to [Frostcores](/items/frostcore) and the [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key) mould chain.

## Tactics

- Clear nearby enemies before searching interiors.
- Use a [Snow Shovel](/items/snow-shovel) to maintain a safe approach.
- Mark searched villages so you do not repeat empty routes.
- Look below the structure instead of assuming the settlement itself is the dungeon.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you find Abandoned Village in Valheim?

Scout visible structures in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north). Then follow the remaining steps above; every required item and preceding location is linked.

### What do you get from Abandoned Village?

Its main verified reward or purpose is shelter, salvage, and access to Winding Tunnels. Exact drop quantities are omitted unless multiple sources agree.

### Is Abandoned Village required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

No. It is useful or optional Deep North content; the direct boss route runs through [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels), [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla), Jotun Invasions, and the Aesir Passage.
