**Abandoned Village is a Deep North poi tied to shelter, salvage, and access to Winding Tunnels.** It fits into the route mapped in the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [locations catalog](/world/locations).

## How to reach it


1. Scout visible structures in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
2. Search the village methodically and identify its largest house.
3. Check beneath that building for a [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) entrance.

## What you get from it

- A route into the first Deep North dungeon.
- Potential shelter and salvage during northern expeditions.
- Access to [Frostcores](/items/frostcore) and the [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key) mould chain.

## Tactics

- Clear nearby enemies before searching interiors.
- Use a [Snow Shovel](/items/snow-shovel) to maintain a safe approach.
- Mark searched villages so you do not repeat empty routes.
- Look below the structure instead of assuming the settlement itself is the dungeon.

## Searching a village efficiently

Treat an Abandoned Village as a surface shell around a possible dungeon route. Clear enemies around the largest structures, search interiors for salvage, and inspect the ground and lower levels for the entrance to [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels). Snow and building debris can hide the approach from a quick shoreline scan.

Place a portal and map marker before descending. The tunnels can be much longer than the village suggests, and the valuable loot—[Frostcores](/items/frostcore), key moulds, and enemy materials—should be banked between branches. A [Snow Shovel](/items/snow-shovel) can keep the path between entrance and portal free of deep-snow slowdown.

Not every village guarantees that a hurried search will reveal the objective. Mark a site as checked only after inspecting the main building footprint and below-ground access points. Keep village salvage secondary to finding three moulds and 20 Frostcores for the main progression route.

Use separate map icons for a village that has been sighted, one whose exterior is cleared, and one with a confirmed tunnel entrance. That small habit prevents repeated searches across similar ruins. If weather closes in, shelter and repair the approach rather than descending with a nearly expired rested bonus.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the Winding Tunnels entrance?

Search the largest village structures and the ground or lower level beneath them. Snow and debris can hide the descent.

### Should I build a portal at the village?

Yes. The dungeon is large, and a nearby portal lets you bank Frostcores and single-use key moulds between branches.
