Enshrouded supports both mouse and keyboard and controllers. You do not need specialized hardware to enjoy it, but comfortable controls, clear co-op audio, and fast storage can make long building and exploration sessions noticeably better.

## What actually matters

- **Controller:** comfortable exploration, building, and relaxed play.
- **Mouse:** faster inventory work and more precise bow, wand, and staff aiming.
- **Headset:** clear voice communication during co-op expeditions and boss fights.
- **SSD space:** Enshrouded currently lists 60 GB of available storage in its PC requirements.

Expensive chairs, capture cards, and ultra-high-refresh competitive displays are not Enshrouded-specific upgrades, so they are outside this guide.
