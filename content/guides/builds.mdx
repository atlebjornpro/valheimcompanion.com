Enshrouded has no locked classes. Any connected skill-tree path is available, so a build is a package of weapon, armor, attributes, skills, food, and team role.

## Melee

Prioritize survivability only until mistakes stop being fatal, then add damage that matches your weapon family. One-handed builds value block and mobility; two-handed and greatsword builds need stamina planning and safe attack windows.

Useful secondary investments include traversal, stamina, and enough ranged utility to deal with flying enemies.

## Ranged

Bow builds reward positioning, stamina, and reliable ammunition supply. Dagger builds are closer-range and benefit more from mobility, critical pressure, and enemy control.

Keep a melee fallback for cramped interiors and a pickaxe for opening alternate routes.

## Magic

Match spell damage types to enemy weaknesses and keep mana regeneration in the complete loadout—not just the skill tree. Wands are flexible and economical; staffs offer larger effects but require charges and longer openings.

Mage armor is less forgiving when surrounded, so movement and line-of-sight are part of the build.

## Co-op

Specialization has more value when the party coordinates:

- A durable front line creates safe cast and shot windows.
- Healing and support skills gain value with consistent grouping.
- Shared food, ammunition, repair access, and traversal tools reduce downtime.

## Build checklist

- Does the weapon match your damage nodes?
- Can the armor survive the content tier after upgrades?
- Do food types reinforce the same plan without duplicating a slot?
- Is stamina or mana regeneration sufficient for a full encounter?
- Can the build handle flying, armored, and resistant enemies?
- Have you kept enough utility points for travel and gathering?

The current maximum is 184 skill points: 88 from levels 2-45, 39 from Shroud Roots, and 57 from 19 Elixir Wells. Track your total with the [Skill Point Calculator](/tools/skill-points).
