Characters begin with **three food slots**. The Dessert Stomach skill unlocks a fourth. Only one effect from each food type can be active at once, so variety matters more than eating four foods with similar names.

## Choose by purpose

- **Meat:** maximum health and, on later meals, damage or defense support.
- **Fruit:** health regeneration.
- **Vegetables and grains:** attribute, stamina, or role-specific support depending on the recipe.
- **Sweets:** usually stamina regeneration; some recipes support mana.
- **Eggs and prepared meals:** mixed benefits that can efficiently cover multiple needs.

## Practical loadouts

For general exploration, use one health-oriented food, one regeneration food, and one stamina or damage food. With Dessert Stomach, add a fourth effect that addresses the region: frost resistance, mana, ranged damage, or another non-duplicating food type.

## Consumables to keep stocked

- Bandages or health potions.
- Shroud Survival Flasks for long Shroud routes.
- Antidotes or regional resistance items when the destination calls for them.
- Wisp of Light or torches for dark interiors.
- Repair Kits only for expeditions where returning to a Workbench is impractical.

Refresh food before entering a dungeon, not after the first fight. A base kitchen is preparation infrastructure, not decoration.
