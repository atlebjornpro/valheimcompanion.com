Enshrouded supports persistent dedicated worlds for groups of up to 16 players. Managed hosting keeps the world available when the original host is offline and removes most of the work involved in updates, ports, backups, and uptime.

## Self-hosting or managed hosting?

**Self-hosting** means running the Enshrouded dedicated-server software on hardware you control, such as a gaming PC, spare computer, or VPS. There is no separate game-hosting subscription, and you keep direct control of the world files and settings. In return, you are responsible for installing and updating the server, configuring ports, scheduling backups, restarting it after problems, and keeping the machine and internet connection available whenever the group wants to play.

Self-hosting is usually the better fit for a small group that already has capable hardware and someone comfortable acting as the server administrator. Follow the [dedicated-server setup guide](/servers/dedicated-server-setup) for the installation and configuration steps, and review the [home-network guide](/guides/best-router-for-enshrouded-self-hosting) before exposing a server from your own connection.

**Managed hosting** means paying a provider to run the server on datacenter hardware. The provider handles the physical machine and network, while you manage the Enshrouded world through a web control panel. Depending on the plan, this can include quick deployment, automatic updates, scheduled backups, DDoS protection, support, and a choice of server regions.

Managed hosting is usually worth considering when the world should stay online around the clock, players live in different regions, or nobody wants to maintain an always-on computer. It costs more than using hardware you already own, and backup retention, file access, support, and renewal prices vary between providers.

Neither option is universally better: choose self-hosting for maximum control and lower ongoing cost, or managed hosting for convenience, predictable uptime, and less administration. The comparison below focuses on the managed options.

## Before choosing

Check these details rather than buying on headline price alone:

1. Pick a datacenter near the majority of players.
2. Confirm the plan supports your intended player count and a growing voxel world.
3. Check whether backups are automatic and how many restore points are retained.
4. Verify that you can download your world before cancelling.
5. Compare the renewal rate—not only the introductory discount.
6. Prefer full file access if you expect to customize settings or migrate later.

For a short campaign, flexible billing may matter most. For a long-running shared build, reliable backups and easy world export are more important than saving a few dollars in the first month.
