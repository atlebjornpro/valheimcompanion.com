This Ranger build emphasizes **weak-point shots, critical hits, and stamina control**. It works best from high ground or behind a co-op front line and keeps a dagger or one-handed weapon for cramped spaces.

## Build summary

| Part | Priority |
|---|---|
| Main attribute | Dexterity |
| Secondary resource | Endurance and stamina regeneration |
| Main weapon | Best upgraded bow available in the current tier |
| Backup | Daggers or a one-handed melee weapon |
| Food | Dexterity, stamina, and stamina regeneration |

## Core skill priorities

Follow connected paths toward:

1. **Marksman** - increases ranged weapon damage.
2. **Dexterity nodes** - raise bow and dagger damage.
3. **Skill Shot upgrades** - improve damage when hitting an enemy weak point.
4. **Sniper** - adds ranged critical-hit chance.
5. **Vitality Surge** - restores stamina after a ranged critical hit.
6. **Ranger** - provides a mixed package of Dexterity, Endurance, stamina recharge, and critical bonuses.
7. **Multi Shot** - gives regular arrows a chance to spawn a spread of extra arrows.

Multi Shot consumes additional regular arrows when it triggers. **Multi Shot Spread** can add another projectile chance without subtracting that added projectile from the ammunition stack. Plan the build around your actual arrow supply rather than a damage test performed with unlimited resources.

## Mobility and sustain

- **Runner** reduces sprint stamina use and raises sprint speed.
- **Rebound** improves base stamina regeneration.
- **Swiftshot Sustenance** can create stamina orbs after bow kills.
- **Airborne** reduces glider stamina consumption.
- **Double Jump** helps secure firing positions and escape melee pressure.

## Combat loop

1. Identify the weak point before drawing.
2. Open from elevation or cover.
3. Move after enemies commit to your previous position.
4. Spend special arrows on groups, armored targets, or control—not routine enemies.
5. Swap weapons when a tight room removes the bow's positioning advantage.

## Equipment checklist

- Upgrade the bow and Ranger armor through the Hunter.
- Carry at least one economical regular-arrow stack.
- Reserve inventory space for specialty arrows.
- Keep enough stamina to dodge after releasing a shot.
- Match damage types and ammunition to the target rather than using one arrow for everything.
