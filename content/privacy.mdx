Enshrouded Companion is a community-built fan site and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Keen Games.

## Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand page views, general device information, approximate region, referrals, and navigation patterns. We do not intentionally collect names, account credentials, or precise location data.

Google may process analytics data according to its own privacy policy. You can limit analytics using browser privacy controls, a tracker blocker, or Google's analytics opt-out tools.

## Local browser data

Interactive tools may store progress and preferences in your browser's local storage. This information remains on your device unless a page explicitly states otherwise. Clearing site data removes it.

## Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you follow one and make a qualifying purchase, Enshrouded Companion may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

This currently includes:

- Amazon Associates product links in the gear and router guides.
- Shockbyte, Nitrado, and Supercraft referral links in the server-hosting comparison.
- Any other outbound link explicitly identified as an affiliate link.

**As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.**

Amazon affiliate links are marked as sponsored links in the page HTML. Product prices, stock, specifications, and promotions can change, so confirm the current details on the seller's page before purchasing.

## Editorial policy

Affiliate availability does not determine the order of recommendations. We aim to recommend products and services only where they fit a real Enshrouded use case, explain when a cheaper option is sufficient, and distinguish direct links from affiliate links.

## Third parties

When you leave this site, the destination provider may set cookies or collect information under its own privacy policy. Relevant providers may include Google, Amazon, Shockbyte, Nitrado, Supercraft, Apex Hosting, and GPORTAL.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the site's tools, analytics, or affiliate relationships change. The date above records the latest revision.
