Valheim Companion does not provide accounts, comments, uploads, advertising, or affiliate tracking.

The hosting provider may process standard request information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, user agent, and diagnostic logs, under its own infrastructure policies.

The site also uses Vercel Web Analytics, a cookieless, aggregate page-view measurement that does not use Analytics-style identifiers and is not affected by the Analytics choice described below.

## Consent-aware Google Analytics

Valheim Companion uses Google Analytics 4 with Advanced Consent Mode. Your country, detected from your network location, determines the starting state:

- **Visitors in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland** start with Analytics and advertising storage **denied**. Analytics storage only turns on after you select **Allow analytics**.
- **Visitors elsewhere** start with Analytics storage **granted** by default, an opt-out model consistent with guidance outside that region. Advertising storage stays denied everywhere regardless of location. You can select **Reject analytics** at any time to turn Analytics storage off.

Before analytics storage is granted, or after it is rejected, Google may still receive consent-state and cookieless measurement requests. These requests do not read or write Analytics cookies.

Cookieless measurements may include the current page address, timestamp, referrer, browser-provided user agent, consent state, and a random number generated for the page load. Google describes this behavior in its [Consent Mode overview](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/concepts/consent-mode).

Advertising storage, advertising user data, and advertising personalization remain disabled regardless of the visitor's Analytics choice.

When Analytics is allowed, Google Analytics may process page addresses, page titles, referral information, approximate location, and browser or device information. It may set first-party cookies beginning with `_ga` to distinguish visits and sessions. The site does not send account details, form content, server credentials, or other intentionally identifying information to Analytics.

The Analytics destination is `G-MZJQXDQC4B`. Google describes its default collection in [Google Analytics data collection](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11593727) and explains its handling of data from partner sites in [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Consent storage and withdrawal

The choice is stored in the browser under the local-storage key `valheim-companion:analytics-consent:v1`. This value records only `granted` or `denied` and is not transmitted to Valheim Companion.

Use **Analytics choices** in the site footer to change or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent prevents further Analytics-cookie storage and attempts to remove Google Analytics cookies for this site. Consent-aware cookieless measurements may continue as described above.
