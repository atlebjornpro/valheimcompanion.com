---
title: Privacy Policy
description: Initial privacy policy for the Valheim Companion scaffold.
updated: 2026-08-04
---

Valheim Companion does not currently provide accounts, comments, uploads, forms, advertising, affiliate tracking, or browser-storage features. No optional analytics integration is enabled in the initial scaffold.

The hosting provider may process standard request information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, user agent, and diagnostic logs, under its own infrastructure policies.

This policy will be updated before enabling analytics, contact forms, affiliate links, personalization, or other data-processing features. The review date above identifies the current version.
