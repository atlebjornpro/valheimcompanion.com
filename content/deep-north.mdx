**The Deep North is live. Valheim 1.0 shipped on September 9, 2026.** This guide covers the content named in Iron Gate's release notes and updating an existing world. Use the [Deep North region reference](/world/regions/deep-north) for a compact in-game checklist.

## What's in the biome

Deep snow needs clearing before building; the release adds the **Snow Shovel** for that work. Iron Gate's Winter Adventures preview documents slippery ice, and the release notes warn about footing on frozen seas and ponds. Treat ice as a travel hazard: the notes do not quantify traction or publish a cracking-ice threshold, so this guide does not give a safe crossing time.

The update also introduces a Grappling Hook, Jotun invasions, and alternate earlier biomes near the world's outer edges. Those are release-wide mechanics, not a promise that every new encounter occurs in the Deep North.

## Creatures and boss

Iron Gate's detailed notes name Barka, Elaking, Eyeless One, Fallen Warrior, Gammeltroll, Hexen, Krigen, Hexahedric Pulp, Shapeless Pulp, Tiny Pulp, Moose, Moose Calf, Seal, Baby Seal, and Writhan. The new boss is **[Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer)**.

This is the update-wide creature list, not a spawn-location table. Moose is explicitly tameable and rideable; see the [taming guide](/resources/taming). Exact health, damage, resistances, and spawn distributions are not given in the cited notes.

## Resources and crafting stations

**Lingonberries** can be harvested in woods and used for food, as documented in Winter Adventures and named again in the release notes. Other named materials include Bloodgold, Frostcore, Frostfire Essence, Thunderblood Essence, Liquid Frost, Ice, Timberwood, Nornathread, Moose Hide, Moose Sinew, Seal Pelt, and Writhan Roots. Their inclusion does not establish particular drops or recipe quantities.

Kale, Oats, Poteitr, and Timberwood are listed as plantable. The notes name the Forge of Potential and item-upgrade mechanics; the [Steam hotfix](/patch-notes/v1-0-12) fixes unintended recipe unlocks there. This is not a complete crafting dependency chart.

## Locations and dungeons

The officially named dungeons are **Winding Tunnels** and **Mörkhalla**. Named locations include an abandoned village, stave hall, Ice Pond, memorial site, and Forge of Potential. The notes do not assign every location to a biome variant, give coordinates, or describe complete loot tables.

## New gear families

The weapon families are **Nord** (the official name, sometimes described as Nordic), **Frostfire**, and **Thunderblood**. The notes list melee and ranged weapons in these families, alongside new staves and shields.

The armor sets are **Protector**, **Vanguard**, **Caller**, and **Lox Fur**. Names alone do not establish resistance values or an optimal boss loadout. The [1.0.10 hotfix](/patch-notes/v1-0-10) fixes repairability of Deep North heavy armor.

## Updating an existing world

Iron Gate's FAQ says existing saves remain usable, but proper new biome generation requires **unexplored areas**. A fresh start is the studio's recommendation. No reliable exploration-radius formula is supplied in the FAQ.

- Back up worlds, characters, launch settings, permission files, and mod configuration before updating.
- Preserve the pre-update copy and test a duplicate; inspect important builds, storage, and portals.
- If you explored northern terrain previously, consider a fresh world for clean generation. Compare the [existing-world options](/servers/existing-world-vs-new-world).
- Update the server and clients, then test joining and saving. Follow the [server update procedure](/servers/updating-a-server).
- Check mod compatibility separately. Review [achievement eligibility](/valheim-1-0/achievements-cheats), including the Steam opt-in introduced by the hotfix.

**Documentation status — September 17, 2026:** the cited Iron Gate notes do not publish a full Kall summoning procedure, attack phases, drops or Forsaken Power, combat statistics, recipe quantities, or complete location loot tables. Those details remain unverified here.
