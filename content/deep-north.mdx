**Deep North is Valheim's final biome**, released with 1.0 on September 9, 2026. Its progression links resource gathering, cast-making, dungeon keys, and Jotun invasions. This guide follows that route from [Fader](/bosses/fader) to [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer); the [region reference](/world/regions/deep-north) provides a compact creature and material checklist.

The route below reflects launch data and the official fixes through **patch 1.0.20**. Later patches changed armour-mould drops, Writhan spawns, terrain persistence, boss-arena spawns, trophies, and the Forge of Potential, so older launch-week guides can now give the wrong expectation.

## Start here: the progression route

1. Land with portal materials, a Snow Shovel, strong food, and a safe coastal fallback.
2. Hunt Seals and use their pelts with Embers to make Ember Charges.
3. Defeat a Gammeltroll, break its petrified remains with a charge, and smelt the Petrified Tissue into Bloodgold.
4. Clear Winding Tunnels for 20 Frostcores and an Intricate Key mould.
5. Build the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry, then cast and finish an Intricate Key.
6. Open Mörkhalla, clear it, and break the Malicious Ice to start a Jotun Invasion.
7. Complete three invasions for three Malicious Blood.
8. Offer the blood at the Aesir Passage and defeat Kall Fimbulbringer.

## What's in the biome

Deep snow needs clearing before construction; bring a **Snow Shovel** to prepare an outpost. Frozen seas and ponds create slippery routes, so leave stamina for a retreat and avoid treating an unfamiliar ice crossing as a safe shortcut. These are good reasons to establish a return route before pushing inland.

Moose can be tamed and ridden. On foot, plan short gathering trips around food, storage, and repair access; the next useful material may require a dungeon rather than another sweep of the surface.

## Creatures and boss

Start by learning which encounters advance your current crafting goal:

| Encounter | Useful materials or role |
|---|---|
| Coastal Seals | Pelts for Ember Charges; also drop Blubber |
| Moose | Meat, Hide, and Sinew; also the biome's rideable animal |
| Gammeltroll | Defeat it, then break the petrified remains with an Ember Charge for Petrified Tissue |
| Elaking in [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) | [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key) moulds and [Elaking Hair Bundles](/items/elaking-hair-bundle) |
| Barka | Frozen Branches for equipment |
| Krigen and Hexen in [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) | Vanguard and Caller moulds respectively; Krigen also supply [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal), Hexen [Nornathread](/items/nornathread) |

Patch 1.0.15 made **both Jotun Warrior types drop both medium and heavy armour moulds** and increased Writhan spawning. Patch 1.0.20 also makes starred enemies more valuable trophy targets because trophy chance now rises with star level.

The [full creature reference](/world/regions/deep-north#creatures) adds dungeon enemies and material drops. Kall is reached through the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) after the invasion chain, rather than by simply finding him while exploring.

## Resources and crafting stations

**[Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) starts with a Gammeltroll, not a metal vein.** Use [Fader's Relic and the Eternal Pyre](/items/ember-charge) to collect Embers. Combine Embers with [Seal Pelts](/items/seal-pelt) to make Ember Charges. After defeating a Gammeltroll, break its petrified body with a charge and process the resulting [Petrified Tissue](/items/petrified-tissue) in a Blast Furnace for Bloodgold.

Gather [Frostcores](/items/frostcore) in [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) for the new production stations. Then keep both branches of the workshop supplied:

| Station | Input and output |
|---|---|
| Blast Furnace | Petrified Tissue, with Coal as fuel → Bloodgold |
| Frigid Kiln | 5 [Ice](/items/ice) → 1 [Liquid Frost](/items/liquid-frost) |
| Black Forge | A mould, Bloodgold, and the recipe's other materials → a cast |
| Frost Foundry | A cast, with Liquid Frost as fuel → the finished item, including [Intricate Keys](/items/intricate-key) and cast-based equipment |

Do not stop at making a cast: the foundry is the finishing step. Likewise, owning a foundry is not enough if the kiln has no Ice. Keep a fuel reserve before committing materials to a new weapon or dungeon key.

**[Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) and [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence)** come from Fallen Warriors summoned at [Ancient Altars](/world/locations/ancient-altar) using Memorial Coal. They supply the corresponding weapon upgrades. **Nornathread**, dropped by Hexen, is needed for Caller equipment and magical weapons. Save these materials while clearing Mörkhalla; they support your combat upgrades alongside the main boss route. The optional [Forge of Potential](/world/locations/forge-of-potential) provides a separate late-game upgrade activity.

Patch 1.0.20 closes a possible Forge of Potential exploit. Update before investing rare materials there, particularly on a persistent multiplayer world.

## Locations and dungeons

1. **[Abandoned villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village) and [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels):** search below the village buildings for the first dungeon tier. Gather Frostcores and Intricate Key moulds here.
2. **Make an [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key):** a level 4 Black Forge turns a mould and 5 Bloodgold into the cast; the Frost Foundry finishes it with Liquid Frost.
3. **[Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla):** use the key to enter. Collect armor moulds and crafting materials on the descent, and find the Vegvisir that marks the Aesir Passage. Breaking the Malicious Ice at the bottom starts a Jotun Invasion elsewhere in the world.
4. **[Jotun Invasion](/world/locations/jotun-invasion):** travel to its marked location in Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain, or Plains. The event has no countdown and can sit between 1 and 12 km from the world centre. Once you approach the core, it attempts a spawn every 50 seconds while holding up to six enemies nearby. Break the 1,000-health core for one guaranteed [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood). Repeat the chain for three Blood in total.
5. **[Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage):** offer the three Blood in the Strange Bowl to reach The Prison and Kall. Read the [boss preparation checklist](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer#prep-checklist) before entering.

Ancient Altars are a useful equipment detour: spend the Memorial Coal collected during dungeon runs on encounters that can yield the weapon essences.

## New gear families

**Nord** weapons form the new equipment tier; **Frostfire** and **Thunderblood** provide further weapon options. The cast system connects surface materials and dungeon moulds to the Frost Foundry. The armor families include **Protector, Vanguard, Caller, and Lox Fur**, with Caller equipment and the new staves giving Nornathread a practical use for magic builds.

Choose upgrades for the next encounter. Kall resists several elemental types, so a weapon's physical damage matters even when its name says Frostfire or Thunderblood. See the [boss damage table](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer#health-and-damage-types). The [1.0.10 hotfix](/patch-notes/v1-0-10) also fixes Deep North heavy-armor repairability.

## Updating an existing world

Iron Gate says existing saves remain usable, but proper new biome generation requires **unexplored areas**. A fresh start is the studio's recommendation.

- Preserve a [world and character backup](/servers/world-backup-restore) before updating or changing mods.
- If you already explored the north, compare the [existing-world options](/servers/existing-world-vs-new-world) before planning a long expedition.
- Update server and clients together, then test joining and saving with the [server update procedure](/servers/updating-a-server).
- Review [mod compatibility](/valheim-1-0/mods-compatibility) and [achievement eligibility](/valheim-1-0/achievements-cheats) separately.
