Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Thunderblood Essence comes from Fallen Warriors at Ancient Altars. Plan the trip through the [Deep North guide](/deep-north), use the [item catalog](/items) to track prerequisites, and check [Deep North locations](/world/locations) before leaving your portal.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Spend [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar).
- Defeat the Fallen Warrior and collect its reward.
- Use the essence on the Thunderblood weapon route for the [Kall](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) stage.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Thunderblood Essence in Valheim?

Thunderblood Essence comes from Fallen Warriors at Ancient Altars. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Thunderblood Essence used for?

Its main verified role is Thunderblood weapon upgrades. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Thunderblood Essence required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
