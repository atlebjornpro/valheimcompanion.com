**Thunderblood Essence is a material tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from Fallen Warriors at Ancient Altars, and its main role is Thunderblood weapon upgrades. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## What it's used for


- Spend [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar).
- Defeat the Fallen Warrior and collect its reward.
- Use the essence on the Thunderblood weapon route for the [Kall](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) stage.

## Optional weapon conversion

Summon a Fallen Warrior by offering three [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar). A defeated warrior has a chance to drop one Thunderblood Essence. Since the type is not guaranteed, stock several offerings and treat either essence as useful progress.

One essence and 20 [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) convert an eligible Nord weapon to its Thunderblood version at the Black Forge. Thunderblood Essence is also used for the Lightning Strike cast. This is an expensive optional route beside the three-key boss chain, so protect the first 15 Bloodgold for [Intricate Keys](/items/intricate-key).

[Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) is the alternate Fallen Warrior reward. Choose equipment based on the weapon and effect you will actually use; collecting an essence does not force an immediate conversion.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Thunderblood Essence guaranteed?

No. Fallen Warriors have a chance to drop either altar essence.

### How much Bloodgold does conversion use?

An eligible Nord weapon conversion uses one essence and 20 Bloodgold.

## Farming priorities

The Fallen Warrior may drop Frostfire Essence instead, so prepare for repeated offerings and keep both results. A portal at the Memorial Site turns later attempts into short combat sessions rather than new exploration trips.

Spend 20 Bloodgold only after the three Intricate Key casts are funded. Compare the Thunderblood conversion with the weapon already in use and retain the essence if another component is missing. The item is portal-safe and does not expire, so there is no penalty for delaying the decision until after Kall.

When farming with a group, decide who receives the first conversion before spending shared Bloodgold. One completed weapon is usually more useful than several players holding partial ingredients. Store any unwanted Frostfire result for a later build rather than consuming more coal immediately while equipment is damaged or food is running low.
