Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Lingonberries in Valheim?

Lingonberries comes from harvesting plants in the Deep North. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Lingonberries used for?

Its main verified role is late-game cooking. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Lingonberries required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
