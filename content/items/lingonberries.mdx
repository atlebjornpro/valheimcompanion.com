## What it's used for


- Harvest them while scouting the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Use them as an ingredient in recipes revealed by your upgraded cooking stations.
- Store them at a safe outpost before entering [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels).

## Finding and using the berry

Lingonberries are part of the 1.0 food and taming progression. Current game-data references place harvestable bushes in the Black Forest, so do not limit the search to Deep North snowfields. Bring the first stack north through a portal when preparing a moose enclosure.

They are the taming food for moose. Drop berries inside a secure pen, leave the animal calm and fed, and keep hostile creatures and fire away while taming progresses. A tamed moose can later be ridden with the appropriate saddle; berries do not produce hide, meat, or sinew without slaughter.

Lingonberries also feed upgraded cooking recipes, including Lingon Juice and several 1.0 meals. Reserve a taming stack before cooking the whole harvest. If no moose accepts them, check that it is not alerted and that the berries are reachable on the ground rather than behind an enclosure wall.

## Common mistakes

- Searching only in the Deep North when current game data places berry bushes in the Black Forest.
- Cooking the entire stack before reserving food for a moose pen.
- Dropping berries while the animal is alerted, blocked from reaching them, or close to fire.

Mark several bushes and revisit them as the world respawns forage. For taming, transport a dedicated stack to the enclosure and watch from far enough away for the moose to calm. Once breeding is stable, surplus berries can move into late-game cooking without risking the only taming supply.
