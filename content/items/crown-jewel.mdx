## Turning the boss drop into equipment

The Crown Jewel is a guaranteed reward from [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer). Reach the fight by completing three [Jotun Invasions](/world/locations/jotun-invasion), offering three [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood) at the Strange Bowl, and entering the Prison beyond the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage). Loot the arena carefully after the fight; the jewel is a crafting component, not a wearable item by itself.

Bring the jewel and **five [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold)** to a **level 4 Black Forge** to craft the [Crown of Valheim](/items/crown-of-valheim). The resulting helmet has 50 armor, adds 5% movement speed, and reduces several stamina costs by 20%. Preparing the Bloodgold before the boss lets you craft immediately on returning to base.

Kall has no Forsaken Power. The jewel leads to the Crown, while the separate [Sacrificial Blood](/items/sacrificial-blood) drop is taken to the Sacrificial Stones for the ending sequence. Do not wait for a trophy interaction at the starting stones.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do with the Crown Jewel?

Take it and five Bloodgold to a level 4 Black Forge to craft the Crown of Valheim.

### Does Kall unlock a Forsaken Power?

No. Kall drops the jewel and Sacrificial Blood, but does not provide a Forsaken Power.

## Boss-loot checklist

Clear inventory slots before entering the Prison and inspect the arena after Kall falls. The Crown Jewel and Sacrificial Blood have different destinations: the jewel goes to a level 4 Black Forge, while the blood goes to the Sacrificial Stones.

The jewel has one confirmed crafting purpose, so there is no reason to carry it on later exploration. Portal home, combine it with the five reserved Bloodgold, and store the resulting Crown with repaired endgame gear. If the Black Forge menu still lacks the recipe, pick the materials up again after confirming the station upgrade.
