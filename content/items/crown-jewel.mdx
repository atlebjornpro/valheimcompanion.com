Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Crown Jewel comes from defeating Kall Fimbulbringer. ## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Defeat [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) after the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) chain.
- Combine the jewel with [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) at the required crafting station.
- Craft the [Crown of Valheim](/items/crown-of-valheim).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Crown Jewel in Valheim?

Crown Jewel comes from defeating Kall Fimbulbringer. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Crown Jewel used for?

Its main verified role is crafting the Crown of Valheim. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Crown Jewel required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
