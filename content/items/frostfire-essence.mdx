**Frostfire Essence is a material tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from Fallen Warriors at Ancient Altars, and its main role is Frostfire weapon upgrades. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## What it's used for


- Offer [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar).
- Defeat the summoned Fallen Warrior for a chance at the essence.
- Use it for the matching late-game weapon path; check the unlocked recipe in-game.

## Optional weapon conversion

Offer three [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar) to summon a Fallen Warrior. The warrior has a chance to drop one Frostfire Essence; the drop is not guaranteed, so bring enough coal for repeat attempts rather than planning around a single summon.

At the Black Forge, one essence and 20 [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) convert an eligible Nord weapon to its Frostfire form. The same essence is also used for the Echo Spike cast. Keep the 15 Bloodgold required for three key casts separate before paying for an optional conversion.

Frostfire is one of two altar essence paths. [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence) comes from the same enemy and supports a different elemental route. The altar is not required to summon Kall, so postpone farming if food, keys, or armor are still blocking the main progression chain.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Frostfire Essence guaranteed?

No. A Fallen Warrior has a chance to drop it, so several Memorial Coal offerings may be required.

### Is the essence required for Kall?

No. It is an optional equipment route through the Ancient Altar.

## Farming priorities

One altar attempt costs three Memorial Coal, and the result may be Thunderblood instead. Bank both essence types and avoid treating the alternate drop as a failed run. Repeated summons are faster when the Memorial Site has a portal, cleared sight lines, and a chest for coal.

The 20-Bloodgold conversion cost is greater than a single Intricate Key cast. Complete the 15-bar mandatory reserve first, then decide whether the Frostfire effect justifies the additional Gammeltroll and Blast Furnace work for the weapon you use.

An essence remains useful even when the matching weapon mould has not appeared. Store it with optional upgrade materials and continue the key route. Once the weapon, Bloodgold, and forge requirements are all ready, craft in one session rather than carrying expensive components between the altar, dungeon portal, and workshop.
