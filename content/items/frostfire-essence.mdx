## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Frostfire Essence comes from Fallen Warriors at Ancient Altars. Plan the trip through the [Deep North guide](/deep-north), use the [item catalog](/items) to track prerequisites, and check [Deep North locations](/world/locations) before leaving your portal.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Offer [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal) at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar).
- Defeat the summoned Fallen Warrior for a chance at the essence.
- Use it for the matching late-game weapon path; check the unlocked recipe in-game.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Frostfire Essence in Valheim?

Frostfire Essence comes from Fallen Warriors at Ancient Altars. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Frostfire Essence used for?

Its main verified role is Frostfire weapon upgrades. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Frostfire Essence required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
