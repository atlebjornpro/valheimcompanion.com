**Malicious Blood is a key tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from completing a Jotun Invasion after breaking Malicious Ice, and its main role is opening the Aesir Passage. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish this complete recipe or progression chain.

1. Unlock [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) with an [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key).
2. Destroy the Malicious Ice at the dungeon bottom to start a [Jotun Invasion](/world/locations/jotun-invasion).
3. Travel to the invasion and destroy its ice core to obtain one Malicious Blood.
4. Repeat until you have three, then offer them at the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage).

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Offer three in the Strange Bowl to enter Kall's Prison.
- Use it only for the [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) summon chain.
- Plan for repeated dungeon and event runs rather than a single clear.

## Common mistakes

- Confusing it with [Sacrificial Blood](/items/sacrificial-blood), which drops after the boss.
- Leaving Mörkhalla without noting where the triggered invasion appeared.
