## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Long Claws in Valheim?

Long Claws comes from Eyeless Ones in Winding Tunnels. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Long Claws used for?

Its main verified role is Deep North crafting recipes. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Long Claws required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
