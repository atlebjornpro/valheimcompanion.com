**Petrified Tissue is a material tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from petrified Gammeltroll remains broken with Ember Charges, and its main role is smelting Bloodgold. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish this complete recipe or progression chain.

1. Craft an [Ember Charge](/items/ember-charge) from the Fader-to-Deep-North supply chain.
2. Find petrified Gammeltroll remains in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
3. Use the charge to break the remains, collect tissue, and smelt it into [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold).

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Smelt it in a Blast Furnace to make Bloodgold.
- Use that metal for an [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key) cast and later equipment.
- Carry enough charges for the deposit rather than making repeated return trips.

## Common mistakes

- Treating the remains as an ordinary pickaxe deposit.
- Leaving the Blast Furnace supply chain until after collecting a key mould.
