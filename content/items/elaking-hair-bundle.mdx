## What it's used for


- Explore [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) and defeat Elaking.
- Collect their hair bundles alongside [Frostcores](/items/frostcore).
- Check newly unlocked recipes at your Deep North crafting stations.

## Dungeon drop route

Elaking in the [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) provide Elaking Hair Bundles. Fight from a cleared doorway or chamber so their pressure does not combine with Draugr and skeleton variants deeper in the branch. Loot before marking the room complete.

The bundles feed Deep North crafting, including gear and casts revealed as the Black Forge and foundry chain develops. Bring the first bundle home to register it, then store later drops beside [Long Claws](/items/long-claws) and [Frostcores](/items/frostcore) rather than mixing them with ordinary creature trophies.

A tunnel run should pursue several goals at once: enemy materials, at least 20 Frostcores for both stations, and three single-use Intricate Key moulds. Leave through a portal when weight or durability becomes a risk; a second controlled pass is cheaper than dying with every rare item in an uncleared room.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do Elaking Hair Bundles drop?

They come from Elaking encountered in Winding Tunnels.

### Should I leave after finding one bundle?

No. Use the same clear to collect Frostcores, Long Claws, and three single-use key moulds.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving the dungeon as soon as an Elaking drops hair while key mould branches remain unexplored.
- Fighting in an uncleared intersection where additional dungeon enemies can join.
- Spending the first bundles before checking every newly revealed cast or equipment recipe.

Elaking Hair Bundles are optional crafting stock rather than the item that opens Mörkhalla. If inventory weight or weapon durability forces a choice, bank Frostcores and key moulds first. Mark the room where the Elaking was fought so later farming runs can distinguish an enemy route from an unsearched loot branch.

Wardrobe Shafts can continue producing Elaking until destroyed, which makes an uncleared shaft both a farming opportunity and a retreat hazard. Decide before advancing whether to break it or control the spawn. Never leave an active shaft on the only path back to the entrance portal.
