**Intricate Key is a key tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from a Winding Tunnels mould, Bloodgold, and the Frost Foundry, and its main role is opening Mörkhalla. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish this complete recipe or progression chain.

1. Find an Intricate Key mould in [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels), including from Elaking.
2. At a level 4 Black Forge, combine the mould with five [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) to make the cast.
3. Fuel the Frost Foundry with [Liquid Frost](/items/liquid-frost) and process the cast into the finished key.
4. Use the key to unlock [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla).

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Open Mörkhalla and continue the route to [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood).
- Keep mould, cast, and finished key distinct when checking your inventory.
- Build the kiln and foundry before committing Bloodgold to the cast.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to use the mould directly on Mörkhalla.
- Stopping at the cast instead of processing it in the Frost Foundry.
