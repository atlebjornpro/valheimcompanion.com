**Memorial Coal is a material tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from Krigen in Mörkhalla, and its main role is summoning Fallen Warriors. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## What it's used for


- Collect it while clearing [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla).
- Offer it at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar).
- Defeat the Fallen Warrior for [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) or [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence).

## Building an altar loop

Memorial Coal can drop from Krigen and can also be recovered from features around Mörkhalla. It is a progression offering rather than ordinary smelter fuel, so keep it out of the same chest as normal Coal.

Take **three Memorial Coal** to an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar) at a Memorial Site. The offering summons a Fallen Warrior. Defeating that enemy gives a chance at [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) or [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence), which support the two optional elemental weapon routes.

Clear the altar area and place a nearby portal before spending the coal. A failed fight costs time and may leave the summon guarding the site. Because the essence drop is chance-based, collect more than one offering set during repeated [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) runs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much Memorial Coal does an altar use?

Offer three pieces at an Ancient Altar to summon one Fallen Warrior.

### Can ordinary Coal replace it?

No. Memorial Coal is a separate offering item and should not be used as smelter fuel.

## Common mistakes

- Feeding Memorial Coal to a normal fuel slot because it resembles ordinary Coal.
- Carrying fewer than three pieces to the altar.
- Summoning a Fallen Warrior before clearing nearby creatures or establishing a portal.

Group the coal into sets of three as it enters storage. One set equals one altar fight, which makes the number of available attempts obvious. If the desired essence does not drop, the offering still completed normally; collect another set during later Mörkhalla clears and try again.

Memorial Coal has no role in the Strange Bowl or the Sacrificial Stones. Those interactions use Malicious Blood and Sacrificial Blood respectively. Keeping all three progression items in labeled chests avoids taking the wrong offering on a long portal or sailing route.
