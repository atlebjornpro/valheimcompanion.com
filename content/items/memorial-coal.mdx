Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Memorial Coal comes from Krigen in Mörkhalla. ## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Collect it while clearing [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla).
- Offer it at an [Ancient Altar](/world/locations/ancient-altar).
- Defeat the Fallen Warrior for [Frostfire Essence](/items/frostfire-essence) or [Thunderblood Essence](/items/thunderblood-essence).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Memorial Coal in Valheim?

Memorial Coal comes from Krigen in Mörkhalla. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Memorial Coal used for?

Its main verified role is summoning Fallen Warriors. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Memorial Coal required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
