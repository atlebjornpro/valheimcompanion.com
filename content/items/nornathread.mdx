## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Nornathread in Valheim?

Nornathread comes from Hexen in Mörkhalla. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Nornathread used for?

Its main verified role is Caller armor and magic equipment. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Nornathread required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
