**Nornathread is a material tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from Hexen in Mörkhalla, and its main role is Caller armor and magic equipment. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## What it's used for


- Clear [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) and defeat Hexen.
- Use Nornathread in Caller armor and other magic-focused recipes.
- Pair the dungeon trip with the [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood) progression run.

## Dungeon farming and crafting

Hexen inside [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) drop one to three Nornathread. Control them early in each room rather than letting their attacks overlap with Krigen pressure, then retrieve the lightweight thread before moving deeper.

Nornathread supports Caller armor and other magic-focused Deep North equipment. It is also required for the Standing Loom. Those recipes unlock alongside moulds and other dungeon materials, so the thread may arrive before the complete output appears in a station menu.

Use the same Mörkhalla trip to collect [Memorial Coal](/items/memorial-coal), find the Aesir Passage Vegvisir, and break Malicious Ice. Store thread before traveling to the resulting invasion; the event can spawn far across the map, and there is no reason to risk the dungeon haul during that journey.

## Frequently asked questions

### What drops Nornathread?

Hexen in Mörkhalla drop one to three pieces.

### Is it only an armor material?

No. It supports Caller gear and the Standing Loom among other magic-focused recipes.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving Hexen alive while fighting tougher-looking melee enemies in the same room.
- Expecting the thread alone to reveal a recipe whose mould or station is still missing.
- Carrying the dungeon haul directly into the distant invasion event.

Bank Nornathread at the entrance portal before breaking the final Malicious Ice. The resulting invasion can be across the map, and its one required reward does not depend on keeping the thread in inventory. Organize the material with armor moulds so it is ready when the Standing Loom or Caller recipe becomes available.

If several players share the dungeon, assign one inventory to thread and moulds while another carries ordinary salvage. This reduces accidental drops when weight fills. Count the thread against the exact planned armor pieces before returning, since repeating a locked Mörkhalla requires another finished key.
