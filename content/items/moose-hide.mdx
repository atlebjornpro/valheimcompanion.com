## What it's used for


- Find moose in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Collect hide while gathering [Moose Meat](/items/moose-meat) and [Moose Sinew](/items/moose-sinew).
- Check Black Forge recipes unlocked during the [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) stage.

## Hunting without losing the tameable herd

Moose are both huntable and tameable in Valheim 1.0. Killing an adult yields two to three Moose Hide together with [Moose Meat](/items/moose-meat) and [Moose Sinew](/items/moose-sinew); taming does not hand over those drops. Keep a breeding or riding pair alive before turning every nearby spawn into crafting materials.

Hide supports the Moose Saddle and several Deep North equipment pieces, including Caller and Vanguard gear, as well as furnishings such as the Moose Bedroll. Exact recipes appear as their other components are discovered, so bring the first hide through a portal and inspect the Black Forge and workbench menus.

For efficient farming, mark a herd, secure the coast or snowfield around it, and carry enough weight capacity for all three drop types. Do not mix a material hunt with an unfinished taming attempt: frightened animals and nearby enemies make both jobs slower.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does taming a moose give hide?

No. Hide is a kill drop; preserve breeding or riding animals before harvesting the herd.

### What should I reserve hide for?

Prioritize the Moose Saddle and planned armor before spending it on furniture.

## Common mistakes

- Killing the only nearby animals before securing a tameable pair.
- Expecting a tame to shed hide without slaughter.
- Spending the first pelts on furnishings before checking saddle and armor costs.

Divide a discovered herd into breeding stock and hunting stock. Move hides through a portal after each harvest and keep them with sinew rather than food. The saddle has practical travel value, while armor choices depend on the build; decide those allocations before decorating a permanent northern base.

When weight is tight, leave ordinary stone or wood before abandoning hide. A coastal portal turns several short hunts into a safe stockpile, and the same trip can collect Seal Pelts. Repair weapons between loops so a later Gammeltroll encounter does not catch the party with low durability.
