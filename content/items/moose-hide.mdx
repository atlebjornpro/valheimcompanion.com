## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Moose Hide comes from moose. Plan the trip through the [Deep North guide](/deep-north), use the [item catalog](/items) to track prerequisites, and check [Deep North locations](/world/locations) before leaving your portal.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Find moose in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Collect hide while gathering [Moose Meat](/items/moose-meat) and [Moose Sinew](/items/moose-sinew).
- Check Black Forge recipes unlocked during the [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) stage.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Moose Hide in Valheim?

Moose Hide comes from moose. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Moose Hide used for?

Its main verified role is Deep North equipment recipes. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Moose Hide required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
