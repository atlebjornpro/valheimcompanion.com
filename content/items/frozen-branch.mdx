## What it's used for


- Defeat Barka in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Use Frozen Branch in equipment such as the Nord Greatsword and Spirit Caller.
- Combine surface farming with a trip to [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels).

## Surface farming route

Frozen Branches drop from Barka in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north). Hunt them on the surface with a portal and a marked retreat route; mixing a surface material run with a full dungeon inventory makes recovery harder than necessary.

The branches are used in high-tier weapons and magic equipment, including the Nord Greatsword and Spirit Caller. Pick up the first branch to reveal recipes for which you already know the other components, then compare those costs with the gear needed for the mandatory Kall route.

Combine Barka hunting with searches for moose, seals, Gammeltrolls, and village entrances. Store each material category at the portal between fights. Frozen Branch is useful, but it does not replace the [Frostcores](/items/frostcore), key moulds, or Bloodgold needed to advance through Mörkhalla.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where does Frozen Branch drop?

It drops from Barka on the Deep North surface.

### Does Frozen Branch advance the key route?

No. It supports equipment; Frostcores, moulds, Bloodgold, and finished keys drive dungeon progression.

## Common mistakes

- Searching Winding Tunnels for a material that comes from surface Barka.
- Treating every branch as mandatory and delaying keys or station construction.
- Carrying a full surface haul into a dungeon rather than banking it at the portal.

Plan a daylight loop around marked Barka territory and repair before leaving base. The drop is portal-safe, so transfer it after each circuit. When the matching equipment recipe appears, count the remaining materials first; Frozen Branch alone is not a reason to commit scarce Bloodgold or dungeon moulds.

Night and poor visibility make it easier to pull extra surface enemies into a Barka fight. Use terrain already cleared on the outward route, then return to the portal before durability becomes critical. A consistent short loop produces more usable material than one overextended expedition followed by a difficult corpse recovery.
