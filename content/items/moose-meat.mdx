## What it's used for


- Find moose in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Process the meat through recipes at your upgraded cooking stations.
- Use strong foods before [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) and [Kall](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer).

## Cooking and herd management

An adult moose drops four to six pieces of Moose Meat when killed. Moose can also be tamed and ridden, but a tame does not generate meat automatically; the drop still requires slaughter. Establish the animals you want to keep before harvesting a herd.

The raw meat can be cooked, while upgraded cooking unlocks recipes such as Smoked Moose Meat, Moose Meat in Bread, Moose Meatballs, and Oven Pancakes. Each route uses the meat differently, so compare the health, stamina, duration, and ingredient cost in the live cooking menu before processing a large stack.

Carry the first meat home through a portal to reveal recipes, then store raw and finished food separately. For [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) and Kall, choose a meal set that supports the role you will play rather than assuming the newest meat dish is automatically best. Keep [Moose Hide](/items/moose-hide) and [Moose Sinew](/items/moose-sinew) from the same kill for equipment crafting.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much Moose Meat drops?

An adult moose drops four to six pieces when killed.

### Does a tame moose produce meat?

No. Taming supports riding and breeding; meat still requires slaughter.

## Common mistakes

- Slaughtering the animals intended for taming or breeding.
- Cooking every raw piece before comparing upgraded recipes.
- Judging food only by one stat instead of health, stamina, duration, and regeneration together.

Keep enough raw meat to reveal and test each available preparation. A mixed food loadout often works better than three recipes aimed at the same stat. Store long-duration expedition meals near the Deep North portal and leave breeding animals untouched so later meat runs do not depend on finding a new herd.

Divide cooked food by purpose: exploration needs duration and balanced stamina, while a Mörkhalla room clear may favor a larger health reserve. Make a small test batch before using the whole raw stack. This keeps options open if another recipe needs meat together with lingonberries, bread, or a Poteitr ingredient.
