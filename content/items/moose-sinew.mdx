## What it's used for


- Hunt moose in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Gather it alongside [Moose Hide](/items/moose-hide) and [Moose Meat](/items/moose-meat).
- Use it in recipes revealed through Deep North crafting progression.

## Drop and equipment planning

Adult moose drop two to three Moose Sinew when killed, alongside [Moose Hide](/items/moose-hide) and [Moose Meat](/items/moose-meat). Tamed moose remain useful for riding and breeding; they do not provide sinew unless slaughtered. Preserve a small herd before farming this material heavily.

Sinew is used by Deep North equipment, including the Caller headdress, Moose Cape, Vanguard hood, and a carved chair. The armor pieces also need other dungeon or surface materials, so store sinew near the Black Forge until the corresponding mould or recipe is available.

Because sinew is light and portal-safe, pick it up even when meat weight forces a choice. Mark reliable moose territory and combine the hunt with coastal seal gathering or Gammeltroll searches. That turns one surface trip into food, armor, and Bloodgold preparation without risking dungeon loot in the same inventory.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much Moose Sinew drops?

An adult moose drops two to three pieces when killed.

### What uses Moose Sinew?

Caller and Vanguard equipment, the Moose Cape, and a carved chair use it.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming tame animals passively produce sinew.
- Dropping sinew when meat fills the carry-weight budget.
- Crafting furniture before reviewing armor and cape requirements.

Sinew is lighter and harder to replace than ordinary meat, so prioritize it during an overloaded hunt. Keep a tally beside planned equipment recipes and stop slaughtering once the material target is met. A surviving tameable pair preserves transport and breeding options for future updates or replacement gear.

For multiplayer worlds, agree on the herd split before the hunt. One player can carry food while another takes hide and sinew, then both can portal home without discarding rarer materials. Mark the pen and the wild hunting area separately so a later expedition does not accidentally empty the breeding stock.
