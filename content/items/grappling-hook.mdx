## How to obtain


1. Search Dvergr treasure chests in Infested Mines for the Hook component.
2. Gather ten Yggdrasil Wood, ten Refined Eitr, and four Mandibles.
3. Craft the Grappling Hook at a Forge after the recipe unlocks.

## What it's used for


- Attach to suitable surfaces for vertical movement and recovery.
- Practice before relying on it in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Combine it with planned routes around [Ice Ponds](/world/locations/ice-pond) and steep terrain.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming the complete tool drops from a chest; the chest supplies its Hook component.
- Relying on it without testing stamina and attachment behavior in a safe area.

## Recipe and safe use

The rare Hook component comes from a Dvergr treasure chest in an Infested Mine. Combine **one Hook, 10 Yggdrasil Wood, 10 Refined Eitr, and four Mandibles** at a Black Forge to make the Grappling Hook. The complete tool does not drop from the chest, and it has one quality level with 300 durability.

Using the hook costs 15 stamina. Aim at a valid surface, attach, and use movement to control the pull rather than spending your entire stamina bar at once. Practice on a low wall near base before trusting it over a lethal drop. Food with a healthy stamina component matters more than raw armor during repeated climbs.

In the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north), the tool helps reach high entrances, cross broken terrain, and recover from steep slopes around Mörkhalla. It complements the [Snow Shovel](/items/snow-shovel): the shovel fixes deep-snow routes on the ground, while the hook solves vertical access. Repair before a long expedition and keep a normal walking route available when weather or combat prevents a safe attachment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where does the Hook component come from?

Find it in a Dvergr treasure chest inside an Infested Mine, then craft the tool at a Black Forge.

### How much stamina does grappling use?

A use costs 15 stamina, so practice and keep enough reserve to recover after the pull.
