## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish this complete recipe or progression chain.

1. Search Dvergr treasure chests in Infested Mines for the Hook component.
2. Gather ten Yggdrasil Wood, ten Refined Eitr, and four Mandibles.
3. Craft the Grappling Hook at a Forge after the recipe unlocks.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Attach to suitable surfaces for vertical movement and recovery.
- Practice before relying on it in the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).
- Combine it with planned routes around [Ice Ponds](/world/locations/ice-pond) and steep terrain.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming the complete tool drops from a chest; the chest supplies its Hook component.
- Relying on it without testing stamina and attachment behavior in a safe area.
