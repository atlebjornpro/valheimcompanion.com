**Crown of Valheim is a gear tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from crafting with Kall's Crown Jewel and Bloodgold, and its main role is endgame armor and mobility. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Category | Gear |
| Biome | [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) |
| Recipe | 1 Crown Jewel + 5 Bloodgold at a level 4 Black Forge |
| Stats | 50 armor; 1,000 durability; one quality level |
| Effects | +5% movement; -20% attack, run, swim, and sneak stamina costs |

## How to obtain


1. Defeat [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) for the [Crown Jewel](/items/crown-jewel).
2. Gather [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) through the Ember Charge and tissue chain.
3. Craft the crown at the required late-game station after the recipe appears.

## What it's used for


- Wear it as an endgame head piece with 50 armor.
- It adds 5% movement speed and reduces attack, run, swim, and sneak stamina costs by 20%.
- Use it after the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) finale as a trophy with practical bonuses.

## Recipe and equipment trade-off

Craft the Crown at a **level 4 Black Forge** with Kall's [Crown Jewel](/items/crown-jewel) and **five [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold)**. The jewel is a guaranteed Kall reward, so the five bars are the part you can prepare before entering the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage). Reserve them separately from the 15 Bloodgold needed for the three key casts.

The Crown has **50 armor**, 1,000 durability, and a single quality level. Its effect adds **5% movement speed** and reduces the stamina cost of attacking, running, swimming, and sneaking by **20%**. Those bonuses make it unusually useful for travel and stamina-heavy combat, but compare the fixed armor value with the upgraded helmet you are replacing. Repair it at the Black Forge when durability runs low.

Kall does not provide a Forsaken trophy or Forsaken Power. The Crown is the practical equipment reward from the Crown Jewel, while [Sacrificial Blood](/items/sacrificial-blood) advances the ending. If the recipe is missing after the fight, pick up both the Crown Jewel and Bloodgold and confirm that the Black Forge is upgraded to level 4.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the Crown of Valheim effects?

It has 50 armor, adds 5% movement speed, and reduces attack, run, swim, and sneak stamina costs by 20%.

### Can the Crown be upgraded?

It has one quality level. Repair its 1,000 durability at the Black Forge.

## When to equip it

The movement and stamina effects benefit long runs, repeated weapon attacks, swimming, and stealth approaches. They also make corpse recovery less punishing when the route crosses snow or broken terrain. Carry your previous helmet in the portal outpost until you have compared armor and resistances with the rest of the active set.

The Crown is crafted after Kall, so it does not help with the first boss clear. Prepare the five Bloodgold in advance, but spend the pre-fight inventory on food, meads, arrows, and repairs. After crafting, test stamina use at base to see how the 20% reductions affect the weapon and movement pattern you actually use.

## Common mistakes

- Arriving at a level 3 Black Forge and assuming the Crown recipe is missing or bugged.
- Spending every Bloodgold bar on the three keys and forgetting to reserve five more for the post-fight craft.
- Expecting the Crown Jewel itself to be equippable.
- Waiting for a second quality level when the Crown has only one.

The Crown occupies the head slot, so its bonuses must be weighed against helmet set effects and armor. Keep the displaced helmet instead of discarding it; some encounters may favor a complete set or a different resistance. For long exploration, corpse runs, and stamina-heavy weapon styles, the movement and cost reductions can be more valuable than a small armor difference. For a stationary defensive role, compare the actual damage received before committing to the swap.
