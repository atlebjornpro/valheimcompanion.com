**Crown of Valheim is a gear tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from crafting with Kall's Crown Jewel and Bloodgold, and its main role is endgame armor and mobility. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish this complete recipe or progression chain.

1. Defeat [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) for the [Crown Jewel](/items/crown-jewel).
2. Gather [Bloodgold](/items/bloodgold) through the Ember Charge and tissue chain.
3. Craft the crown at the required late-game station after the recipe appears.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Wear it as an endgame head piece with 50 armor.
- Community sources agree it adds 5% movement speed and reduces several stamina costs by 20%.
- Use it after the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) finale as a trophy with practical bonuses.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Crown of Valheim in Valheim?

Follow the crafting or progression chain above. The key prerequisites are linked in each step, ending with crafting with Kall's Crown Jewel and Bloodgold.

### What is Crown of Valheim used for?

Its main verified role is endgame armor and mobility. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Crown of Valheim required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
