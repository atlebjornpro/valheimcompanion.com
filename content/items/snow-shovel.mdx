**Snow Shovel is a tool tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from crafting at a level 3 Black Forge, and its main role is clearing deep snow. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish this complete recipe or progression chain.

1. Gather five Finewood, four Flametal, and two Embers.
2. Upgrade a Black Forge to level 3.
3. Craft the shovel and take it to the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Clear deep snow for routes and outpost construction.
- Prepare approaches to [Abandoned Villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village).
- Keep a safe path to [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) entrances.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Snow Shovel in Valheim?

Follow the crafting or progression chain above. The key prerequisites are linked in each step, ending with crafting at a level 3 Black Forge.

### What is Snow Shovel used for?

Its main verified role is clearing deep snow. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Snow Shovel required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
