**Snow Shovel is a tool tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from crafting at a level 3 Black Forge, and its main role is clearing deep snow. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## How to obtain


1. Gather five Finewood, four Flametal, and two Embers.
2. Upgrade a Black Forge to level 3.
3. Craft the shovel and take it to the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north).

## What it's used for


- Clear deep snow for routes and outpost construction.
- Prepare approaches to [Abandoned Villages](/world/locations/abandoned-village).
- Keep a safe path to [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) entrances.

## Recipe and route building

Craft the Snow Shovel at a **level 3 Black Forge** with **five Finewood, four Flametal, and two Embers**. Picking up the relevant materials reveals the recipe; the Embers come from the Eternal Pyre created after Fader. The tool uses five stamina per action and gains durability through its upgrades.

Use it on deep snow to remove the movement penalty and expose a dependable path. Clear a strip between a portal, workshop, and the entrance to an [Abandoned Village](/world/locations/abandoned-village) or [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels). A narrow marked lane costs less durability than clearing an entire field and is easier to recognize in a storm.

The shovel changes terrain access rather than harvesting a special snow resource. Repair it before a long scouting loop, and clear routes while the area is safe; trying to shovel during a creature attack spends the same stamina needed to block or escape. Builders can also use it to prepare flatter, readable spaces around a northern outpost.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Snow Shovel recipe?

At a level 3 Black Forge, combine five Finewood, four Flametal, and two Embers.

### Does the shovel gather Snow?

Its main job is clearing deep snow and its movement penalty to create safe routes and building space.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting the shovel to mine Ice; that requires a Black Metal pickaxe.
- Clearing a broad field when one narrow portal-to-entrance lane is enough.
- Spending the last stamina on terrain work while enemies are nearby.

Carry the shovel repaired and use short passes between safe points. The cleared route remains most valuable where repeated trips are planned: a kiln camp, village entrance, dungeon portal, or corpse-recovery lane.
