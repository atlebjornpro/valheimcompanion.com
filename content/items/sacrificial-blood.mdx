**Sacrificial Blood is a key tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from defeating Kall Fimbulbringer, and its main role is Valheim's ending at the Sacrificial Stones. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Sacrificial Blood comes from defeating Kall Fimbulbringer. Plan the trip through the [Deep North guide](/deep-north), use the [item catalog](/items) to track prerequisites, and check [Deep North locations](/world/locations) before leaving your portal.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Defeat [Kall Fimbulbringer](/bosses/kall-fimbulbringer) in the Prison.
- Carry the blood back to the Sacrificial Stones to trigger the ending.
- It is separate from [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), the pre-fight offering.

## Common mistakes

- Taking it to the [Aesir Passage](/world/locations/aesir-passage) instead of the Sacrificial Stones.
- Expecting it to grant a Forsaken Power; Kall does not grant one.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Sacrificial Blood in Valheim?

Sacrificial Blood comes from defeating Kall Fimbulbringer. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Sacrificial Blood used for?

Its main verified role is Valheim's ending at the Sacrificial Stones. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Sacrificial Blood required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
