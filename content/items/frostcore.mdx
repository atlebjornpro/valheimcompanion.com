## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Build the Frigid Kiln that processes [Ice](/items/ice).
- Build the Frost Foundry that finishes [Intricate Keys](/items/intricate-key) and Deep North casts.
- Treat the first cores as progression materials before optional upgrades.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Frostcore in Valheim?

Frostcore comes from Winding Tunnels. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Frostcore used for?

Its main verified role is the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Frostcore required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
