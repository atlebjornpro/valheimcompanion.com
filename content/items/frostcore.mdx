**Frostcore is a material tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from Winding Tunnels, and its main role is the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## What it's used for


- Build the Frigid Kiln that processes [Ice](/items/ice).
- Build the Frost Foundry that finishes [Intricate Keys](/items/intricate-key) and Deep North casts.
- Treat the first cores as progression materials before optional upgrades.

## First pickup and station budget

Search the [Winding Tunnels](/world/locations/winding-tunnels) thoroughly for Frostcores embedded through the dungeon environment. The first pickup unlocks both Deep North processing stations, so bring inventory space and continue collecting after the recipe notification. You need **20 cores total** to build one of each station.

The **Frigid Kiln** costs 10 Frostcore, 20 Stone, and 5 [Ice](/items/ice). It processes five Ice into one [Liquid Frost](/items/liquid-frost), the coolant used as Frost Foundry fuel. The **Frost Foundry** costs another 10 Frostcore, 15 Iron, and 20 Stone. It turns prepared casts, including the [Intricate Key](/items/intricate-key) cast, into finished items.

Portal the cores out as soon as a branch is secure. Stone and Iron can be staged later, but losing the first ten cores delays every cast. If a run yields fewer than 20, mark cleared rooms and return for unexplored walls, floors, and side chambers instead of assuming the first visible cluster is the dungeon's entire supply.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many Frostcores do both stations need?

Twenty total: ten for the Frigid Kiln and ten for the Frost Foundry.

### What does the first Frostcore unlock?

Picking one up reveals the Frigid Kiln and Frost Foundry recipes.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving after ten cores and forgetting that the kiln and foundry each need ten.
- Spending Ice on kiln construction without mining enough extra for its first fuel cycle.
- Building the stations at an exposed northern camp when the portal can carry all ingredients home.

The two stations form one system. The kiln makes Liquid Frost; the foundry consumes it while finishing casts. Build them close enough that processed fuel, casts, and finished keys can share secure storage. Neither station replaces the level 4 Black Forge step that creates the cast.
