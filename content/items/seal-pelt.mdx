## How to obtain

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Seal Pelt comes from seals on the Deep North coast. Plan the trip through the [Deep North guide](/deep-north), use the [item catalog](/items) to track prerequisites, and check [Deep North locations](/world/locations) before leaving your portal.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Hunt seals along the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) coast.
- Combine the pelt with Embers to make an [Ember Charge](/items/ember-charge).
- Use the charge on petrified Gammeltroll remains for [Petrified Tissue](/items/petrified-tissue).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Seal Pelt in Valheim?

Seal Pelt comes from seals on the Deep North coast. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Seal Pelt used for?

Its main verified role is Ember Charges. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Seal Pelt required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
