**Seal Blubber is a consumable tied to Deep North progression.** You obtain it from seals on the Deep North coast, and its main role is Deep North cooking and crafting. Use this page alongside the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [item catalog](/items).

## What it's used for


- Hunt seals on the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) coast.
- Keep blubber separate from [Seal Pelt](/items/seal-pelt), which is used for Ember Charges.
- Check the upgraded Cauldron recipes before a [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) expedition.

## Food use and storage

Seals on the Deep North coast drop blubber separately from [Seal Pelts](/items/seal-pelt). Adult seals normally provide two to three pieces; the blubber stacks to 20 and is portal-safe, so bank it rather than consuming the first drop during the hunt.

Raw Seal Blubber is edible, but cooking it in a Stone Oven produces Cooked Seal Blubber and makes better use of the ingredient. It also feeds late-game recipes including Seal Soup and Unbaked Poteitr. Check the upgraded Cauldron and oven after picking it up so the recipes appear in your crafting list.

Blubber is a food ingredient, not the material used for Ember Charges. The visually related Seal Pelt fills that progression role. Store the two drops in separate chests, especially when preparing a [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) run: blubber supports the food loadout, while pelts should remain available for charges and equipment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Seal Blubber used for Ember Charges?

No. Ember Charges use Seal Pelts; blubber is a food ingredient.

### Can Seal Blubber be cooked?

Yes. A Stone Oven makes Cooked Seal Blubber, and upgraded cooking uses it in later recipes.

## Common mistakes

- Confusing blubber with the Seal Pelt required for Ember Charges.
- Eating the first raw pieces before discovering oven and Cauldron recipes.
- Hunting from a position where the corpse or drops can fall into unreachable water.

Approach coastal seals with retrieval in mind and leave inventory weight for both drop types. Portal the blubber home and pick it up near the upgraded cooking stations to refresh available recipes. Cook only the amount required for the chosen meal plan; raw stock remains more flexible for later recipes.
