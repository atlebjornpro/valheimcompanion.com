Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish a complete drop table. Seal Blubber comes from seals on the Deep North coast. Plan the trip through the [Deep North guide](/deep-north), use the [item catalog](/items) to track prerequisites, and check [Deep North locations](/world/locations) before leaving your portal.

## What it's used for

Community-verified; Iron Gate's notes do not publish every recipe detail.

- Hunt seals on the [Deep North](/world/regions/deep-north) coast.
- Keep blubber separate from [Seal Pelt](/items/seal-pelt), which is used for Ember Charges.
- Check the upgraded Cauldron recipes before a [Mörkhalla](/world/locations/morkhalla) expedition.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get Seal Blubber in Valheim?

Seal Blubber comes from seals on the Deep North coast. The exact quantity is omitted because the available sources do not establish one consistent amount.

### What is Seal Blubber used for?

Its main verified role is Deep North cooking and crafting. The linked outputs above show where it sits in the Deep North progression chain.

### Is Seal Blubber required for Kall Fimbulbringer?

It is not itself the final offering. The direct offering is [Malicious Blood](/items/malicious-blood), obtained through Mörkhalla and Jotun Invasions.
