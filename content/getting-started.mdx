## The first hour

Leave the Cinder Vault, collect **twigs, stone, plant fiber, and wood logs**, then craft an axe, pickaxe, club, bow, bandages, and a construction hammer. Accept the Flame's base quest and place a Flame Altar on open ground above the Shroud.

Your first base only needs four things:

1. A roofed room with a heat source and a bed.
2. A Workbench, which also repairs equipped gear.
3. Storage beside the places where you craft.
4. Space for rescued Survivors and their stations.

> **Good first-base rule:** choose convenience over permanence. Flame Altars become fast-travel points, and you can build a grander home once you understand the terrain and production chains.

## The progression loop

Enshrouded progression is deliberately interlocked:

- **Explore** to reach Ancient Vaults, Flame Shrines, Shroud Roots, and Elixir Wells.
- **Rescue Survivors** to unlock specialist crafting and quests.
- **Strengthen the Flame** to cross stronger Shroud and enter later regions.
- **Upgrade gear** and spend skill points to make the next expedition safer.
- **Improve the base** so preparation takes less time.

## Rescue order

Prioritize the five foundational craftspeople in the Springlands: **Blacksmith, Alchemist, Hunter, Carpenter, and Farmer**. Their quests unlock the core metalworking, alchemy, leather, wood, farming, and cooking chains.

The Collector, Bard, Barber, and Fisher expand the base and open specialist systems later. Assistants duplicate a craftsperson's services at another base.

## Before every expedition

- Refresh the **Rested** buff under shelter with warmth and comfort.
- Carry food from different food types so their effects can coexist.
- Bring a pickaxe, felling axe, ranged option, bandages or potions, and enough inventory space.
- Place a spare Flame Altar as a temporary fast-travel anchor when exploring far from home.
- Mark mines, farms, vaults, and blocked Shroud passages on the map.

## Your first milestones

| Milestone | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Workbench | Repairs gear and unlocks foundational crafting |
| Grappling Hook | Opens traversal routes and tower puzzles |
| Glider | Makes towers and long-distance travel dramatically faster |
| Five core Survivors | Unlocks the main crafting economy |
| Flame level 3 | Opens safer progression into Revelwood |
| Magic Chests | Let nearby crafting stations draw materials from storage |
