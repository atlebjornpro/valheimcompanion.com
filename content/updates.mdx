**Valheim 1.0 is released.** This page records review status and acknowledged issues as of September 17, 2026. The [patch-notes archive](/patch-notes) holds the individual release summaries.

## Current release status

| Announcement | Published | Scope in the announcement |
|---|---|---|
| [1.0 release](/patch-notes/v1-0-release) | September 9, 2026 | Full release and Deep North |
| [1.0.10 hotfix](/patch-notes/v1-0-10) | September 11, 2026 | All platforms; Xbox label 1.0.11 |
| [1.0.12 hotfix](/patch-notes/v1-0-12) | September 11, 2026 | Steam only; crossplay compatible |

These labels describe the cited announcements. Check your platform's changelog for subsequent rollout rather than assuming identical version numbers.

## Acknowledged issues and fixes

Iron Gate's September 11 notes report fixes for Deep North heavy armor repair, Shaman damage and healing, snow behavior, and sound. The Steam patch addresses unwanted Forge of Potential unlocks, a zone-loading crash, permission IDs, and achievement messaging.

**Item stands still need care:** the Steam fix is conditional. Iron Gate advises backups before removing valuable items from stands encountered since installing 1.0. Read the [hotfix details](/patch-notes/v1-0-12) before doing so.

## Guide review status

The Deep North, region, boss, release, and achievements pages now reference the published notes. Exact Kall combat statistics, summoning quantities, drops, and Forsaken Power remain unverified because the cited notes do not provide them. Console-command names are confirmed; individual descriptions require the installed game's help output.

Mod compatibility and platform-specific hardware performance are not established by these notes. This site has not performed a full game or dedicated-server playtest for this editorial batch.

## Updating a server

Preserve a complete backup, update the application, and test loading, joining, permission lists, and saving. The [server update guide](/servers/updating-a-server) covers the procedure; [connection troubleshooting](/servers/server-not-showing) covers post-update join failures.
