## Self-host vs managed hosting

**Self-hosting** means running `valheim_server.exe` or `valheim_server.x86_64` on your own hardware or a VPS you control. You avoid a hosting bill, but you handle updates, port forwarding, backups, security, and uptime. The [dedicated server setup guide](/servers/dedicated-server-setup) covers the process, and the [configuration generator](/servers/server-settings) builds the launch command.

**Managed hosting** puts the server on a provider's hardware and gives you a web control panel, automated updates and backups, DDoS protection, and a choice of datacenter. It is the simpler option when nobody in the group wants to maintain a machine.

## Crossplay matters for which backend you pick

Valheim's dedicated server can use the **Steam backend** or the **PlayFab crossplay backend** (`-crossplay`). PlayFab is required when your group spans PC and console platforms. A host may support crossplay without enabling it by default, so confirm the setting before inviting players. The [crossplay guide](/servers/crossplay) explains the practical differences.
