**Valheim 1.0 launches September 9, 2026.** The full release centers on the Deep North, adds PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 versions, and supports crossplay between all announced platforms.

This page separates confirmed launch information from details that Iron Gate has not yet published.

## Confirmed platforms and storefronts

At launch, Valheim 1.0 is planned for:

- Steam on Windows, Linux, and Mac;
- the Mac App Store;
- Humble Store on Windows, Linux, and Mac;
- Microsoft Store on Windows;
- Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S;
- PlayStation 5;
- Nintendo Switch 2.

The announcement does not include the original Nintendo Switch or PlayStation 4.

## Crossplay compatibility

Iron Gate confirms full crossplay between all launch platforms. A crossplay-capable dedicated server uses the PlayFab backend, selected with the `-crossplay` launch argument. Steam-backend dedicated servers remain limited to Steam users.

| Player platform | All-platform crossplay announced? | Cross-platform save sync announced? |
|---|---:|---:|
| Steam: Windows, Linux, Mac | Yes | No general sync announced |
| Microsoft Store: Windows | Yes | Sync with Xbox saves |
| Xbox One / Series X|S | Yes | Accessible on PC through the Microsoft Store version |
| PlayStation 5 | Yes | No |
| Nintendo Switch 2 | Yes | No |
| Mac App Store | Yes | No general sync announced |

Crossplay means players can join one another; it does not mean every storefront shares purchases or save files. See the [full crossplay and server guide](/servers/crossplay).

## Deep North

The Deep North is the final biome update that brings Valheim to version 1.0. Iron Gate has previewed wintry terrain and systems including deep snow, slippery ice, harvestable lingonberries, a shovel, the Frost Foundry, a loom extension for the Galdr table, a drawbridge, and 67-degree roof pieces.

The release announcement also confirms new enemies, weapons, and building opportunities without publishing complete statistics or progression requirements. Those details remain outside this guide until official patch notes or the released game can be checked.

Read the [Deep North preparation guide](/deep-north) for a source-by-source breakdown.

## Existing saves and world generation

Old characters and worlds remain usable. New biome generation works properly only in areas that have not been explored, and Iron Gate recommends starting from scratch for the best complete experience.

That creates a real choice for established communities: preserve bases and history, or remove uncertainty around new biome generation. Use the [existing world versus new world guide](/servers/existing-world-vs-new-world) before launch.

## Achievements and cheats

Valheim 1.0 introduces achievements, with more than 50 previewed by Iron Gate. Tracking begins when 1.0 is installed, so past actions do not automatically award achievements.

Most developer commands can flag a character and world against achievements, including command use from before 1.0. Spawned items are marked, and some world modifiers also affect eligibility. The official FAQ provides the authoritative rules and should be reviewed before reusing a cheat-enabled save.

## Mods at launch

Valheim has no official mod support. Iron Gate cannot guarantee that existing mods will work on launch day, and 1.0 will not include Steam Workshop or console mod support.

Back up modded saves, record the current mod list, and wait for each mod author's compatibility statement. A base-game update and a mod migration should be tested as separate changes whenever possible.

## Price at full release

The announced US base price is **$29.99**, with regional differences possible. Existing Early Access owners do not need to purchase the game again; their copy updates normally.

## Dedicated servers

Valheim 1.0 remains a 1–10 player game. Iron Gate's current dedicated-server application supports Windows and Linux launch scripts, Steam or PlayFab networking, existing-world selection, automatic backups, visibility controls, permission files, and world modifiers.

Launch-day application changes have not yet been documented, so server owners should preserve their existing launch file and world data before updating. Continue with the [Valheim 1.0 dedicated-server setup](/servers/dedicated-server-setup) and [server update checklist](/servers/updating-a-server).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a new world?

No. Existing worlds remain usable, but proper biome generation is limited to unexplored areas and Iron Gate recommends restarting.

### Can I go directly to the Deep North?

Yes, according to the official FAQ, although Iron Gate again recommends starting over.

### Will PS5, Xbox, Switch 2, and PC play together?

Yes. Full crossplay is confirmed between all announced platforms.

### Does crossplay include save progression?

No general cross-platform save sync is planned. The stated exception is Xbox saves accessed on PC through the Microsoft Store version.

### Will mods work on launch day?

Possibly, but Iron Gate does not guarantee compatibility and provides no official mod support.
