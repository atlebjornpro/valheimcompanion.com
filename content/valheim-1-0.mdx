Valheim 1.0 is scheduled for **September 9, 2026**. Iron Gate has announced that the release includes the Deep North and is planned for PC, Linux, Mac, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2 with full crossplay.
