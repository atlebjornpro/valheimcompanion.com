Valheim releases for **Nintendo Switch 2 on September 9, 2026**, the same day as Valheim 1.0 and the Deep North. It is a Switch 2 release and is **not coming to the original Nintendo Switch**.

Iron Gate confirms full crossplay with every other supported platform, plus Switch 2-specific mouse mode, touch-screen menu support, advanced vibration, and gyroscope aiming.

## Is Valheim coming to the original Switch?

No. Iron Gate explicitly says the release is for Nintendo Switch 2, not the first Nintendo Switch console. A Nintendo Switch 2 game listing should not be interpreted as a backwards-compatible original-Switch release.

The Nintendo product page lists handheld, tabletop, and TV modes for the Switch 2 version.

## Does Switch 2 support Valheim crossplay?

Yes. Switch 2 players can play with supported PC, Mac, Linux, Xbox, and PlayStation 5 players. Iron Gate describes the 1.0 release as full crossplay between all platforms.

For a persistent shared world, the group can use a crossplay-enabled dedicated server. The server itself runs on supported computer hardware or with a managed provider; Switch 2 players join it as clients.

The official Nintendo listing indicates that online play uses Nintendo Switch Online features. Confirm the subscription requirements in your region before organizing a multiplayer group.

## Switch 2 controls and platform features

Iron Gate announced four features specifically for the Switch 2 port:

- **mouse mode**;
- **touch-screen support for menus**;
- **advanced vibration**;
- **gyroscope aiming**.

The announcement does not publish performance targets, resolution modes, or detailed graphics comparisons. Those should be tested on the retail build rather than inferred from trailer footage.

## Can Switch 2 share saves with PC, Xbox, or PS5?

Iron Gate says cross-platform save access is limited to Xbox and the Microsoft Store version on PC. Beyond that pairing, there is no cross-platform save synchronization.

Crossplay and cross-save are different features. A Switch 2 player can join friends on another platform without gaining an automatic local copy of their world. For a persistent group, decide which machine or provider owns the authoritative server world and maintain backups there.

## Does Switch 2 support mods?

No. Iron Gate says Valheim 1.0 will not support mods on consoles. PC mods are unofficial as well, and a modded PC server is not guaranteed to remain compatible with console clients.

Use a clean server for a mixed Switch 2 group unless every server-side change has been specifically verified not to require console-side files.

## Player count and local multiplayer

Valheim remains a 1–10 player game. Nintendo's product listing shows **one player on a single system** and online play for up to 10 players. It does not list local split-screen multiplayer.

For two people in the same room to play together, plan on separate supported systems and accounts unless Nintendo or Iron Gate announces another mode.

## Download-size caution

Iron Gate estimates a 1.0 download of about **4.3 GiB on most platforms**, while the pre-release Nintendo storefront currently displays a much smaller download-size figure in some regions. Those figures do not align, so this guide does not present the storefront number as the final retail download.

Leave more than 4.3 GiB free and check the eShop listing on launch day. Updates can require additional temporary storage while installation completes.
