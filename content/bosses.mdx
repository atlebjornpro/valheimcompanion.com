Every biome in Valheim ends with a boss fight, and every boss fight gates the gear needed for the next biome. This guide covers all seven bosses released as of today, in progression order, with what each one actually does in the fight — not just how to summon it.

## Boss progression

| 7 | [Fader](/bosses/fader) | [Ashlands](/world/regions/ashlands) | 3 Bells |

Every fight (except The Queen's first encounter) requires sacrificing an item at a biome-specific altar, usually found via a Vegvísir picked up somewhere in that biome. Each boss drops a trophy — mount it to activate a temporary Forsaken Power buff — plus a progression item that unlocks the next tier of gear or crafting.

> **Heads up:** this covers the seven bosses released today. Valheim 1.0 adds an eighth biome, the Deep North, on September 9, 2026 — see the [1.0 release hub](/valheim-1-0) for what's confirmed so far.

## Early bosses: learning the pattern

[Eikthyr](/bosses/eikthyr) is a low-stakes tutorial fight with starting gear. [The Elder](/bosses/the-elder) is the first fight that rewards real prep — fire arrows turn it from a slog into a quick kill. [Bonemass](/bosses/bonemass) introduces damage-type matchups seriously: Blunt weapons and Poison Resistance Mead matter here in a way they haven't before.

## Mid game: gear checks

[Moder](/bosses/moder) shifts the fight toward ranged play since she spends much of it airborne. [Yagluth](/bosses/yagluth) is widely described as a genuine difficulty spike — fire, lightning, and a punishing ground-slam attack call for dedicated Fire Resistance prep.

## Endgame

[The Queen](/bosses/the-queen) introduces an escalating fight structure, gaining attacks as her health drops, with real add management to juggle alongside the boss herself. [Fader](/bosses/fader) is the current final boss — a long, fire-heavy endurance fight in Ashlands' coliseum arena, and the top of the released progression ladder until Deep North arrives.
