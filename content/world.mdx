Valheim's world is built from distinct biomes, each harder than the last, each gated by the boss of the one before it. This guide covers the seven biomes released as of today, in the order most players progress through them — what lives there, what to gather, and how to beat the boss that unlocks the next one.

## Biome progression

| Order | Biome | Boss | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Meadows](/world/regions/meadows) | Eikthyr | Antler Pickaxe (mining) |
| 2 | [Black Forest](/world/regions/black-forest) | The Elder | Swamp Key |
| 3 | [Swamp](/world/regions/swamp) | Bonemass | Wishbone (find Silver) |
| 4 | [Mountain](/world/regions/mountain) | Moder | Artisan Table, Spinning Wheel, Blast Furnace |
| 5 | [Plains](/world/regions/plains) | Yagluth | Elemental resistance, Mistlands readiness |
| 6 | [Mistlands](/world/regions/mistlands) | The Queen | Mining/Eitr boost, Ashlands readiness |
| 7 | [Ashlands](/world/regions/ashlands) | Fader | Combat/movement boost, current endgame |

Each fight requires an item sacrifice at a biome-specific altar — usually found via a Vegvísir rune stone picked up somewhere in that biome — and drops both a trophy (for a temporary Forsaken Power buff) and a progression item that gates the next tier of gear.

> **Heads up:** this order covers the seven biomes released today. Valheim 1.0 adds an eighth biome, the Deep North, on September 9, 2026 — see the [Deep North guide](/deep-north) and the [1.0 release hub](/valheim-1-0) for what's confirmed so far.

## Starting out

New worlds always begin in [Meadows](/world/regions/meadows), the lowest-risk biome and the best place for a first base. From there, most players clear [Black Forest](/world/regions/black-forest) for Copper and Tin before pushing toward [Swamp](/world/regions/swamp) for Iron — the biome most guides treat as the first real difficulty spike.

## Mid and late game

[Mountain](/world/regions/mountain) introduces cold as a hazard as much as combat, gated behind the Wishbone from Bonemass. [Plains](/world/regions/plains) is notoriously dangerous for the gear tier available when players first arrive — Deathsquitos alone catch out plenty of well-armored characters. [Mistlands](/world/regions/mistlands) unlocks Eitr and the game's magic system behind a permanent, disorienting mist.

## Endgame

[Ashlands](/world/regions/ashlands) is the current final released biome — a lava-scarred zone built around fire hazards, Flametal gear, and the boss Fader. It remains the top of the released progression ladder until Deep North arrives.
