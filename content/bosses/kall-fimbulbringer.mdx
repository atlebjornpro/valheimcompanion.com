## The fight

Iron Gate names the boss but does not describe attacks, phase transitions, health, damage, or resistances in the cited release notes as of September 17, 2026. This guide cannot yet support a phase-by-phase strategy or an elemental weakness recommendation.

Kall's drops, trophy effects, and Forsaken Power are not published in the cited Iron Gate notes as of September 17, 2026. New items in the release list are not evidence that Kall drops them.

## Prep checklist

- Back up the character and world before testing an unfamiliar encounter.
- Review the [Deep North gear overview](/deep-north#new-gear-families). Nord, Frostfire, and Thunderblood weapons and Protector, Vanguard, Caller, and Lox Fur armor are listed. This does not establish the best loadout for Kall.

## Arena and terrain

The announcement lists Winding Tunnels and Mörkhalla as dungeons, but does not identify either as Kall's arena. No arena hazard or summoning-location claim is made here. Snow and ice hazards are covered in the [region reference](/world/regions/deep-north).
