**Moder is Valheim's fourth boss**, fought in [Mountain](/world/regions/mountain). She's the first fight built around a flying enemy — expect more ranged play than earlier bosses. See [how this site sources boss guides](/data-sources#boss-fight-guides).

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Biome | [Mountain](/world/regions/mountain) |
| Summon item | 3 Dragon Eggs (heavy, can't travel through portals) |
| Altar | Moder's altar, within the Mountain |
| Drops | Dragon Tears, 1 Moder Trophy |
| Forsaken Power | Tailwind while sailing |
| Recommended gear tier | Wolf armor / Silver |

## The fight

Moder alternates between two states throughout the fight:

- **Airborne:** she circles and hovers, raining down a frost breath barrage from range. She's highly mobile in this state and won't commit to a single attack line — dodge, roll, or use terrain for cover.
- **Grounded:** her breath becomes a continuous jet in a straight line, backed by melee-range wing swings, claw swipes, and a bite. This is when she's slow to reposition — the best window to close in and melee.

**Important quirk:** Moder is immune to Frost damage herself, so Frostner and other frost-based weapons perform poorly here despite being strong elsewhere. Her only confirmed elemental weakness is Fire, though sources disagree on whether Fire or Obsidian arrows deal more total damage.

## Prep checklist

- **Ranged weapon:** a Draugr Fang bow is the consistent top pick, with a large arrow stockpile (200-300+) as your primary damage plan given how much of the fight she spends airborne.
- **Melee backup:** a Silver Sword and Silver Shield for grounded windows — not Frostner.
- **Armor:** Wolf armor set, ideally upgraded, for both frost resistance and physical mitigation.
- **Frost Resistance Mead:** as a supplement to Wolf armor's built-in resistance.
- **Food:** a mix of high-HP and stamina foods — Serpent Stew, Lox Meat, Sausages, or Queen's Jam are common picks.
- Enter the fight Rested if possible.

## Arena and terrain

The altar/summoning platform is indestructible and can double as cover from her aerial attacks. Natural terrain and any wooden structures get destroyed quickly, so they're not reliable long-term cover — raising earthen pillars or digging trenches with a Hoe beforehand gives more durable protection. Clear nearby Stone Golems, Drakes, and wolves before engaging, and avoid starting the fight at night, when more Mountain creatures are active.

## Common mistakes

- Bringing Frostner as a main weapon — her frost immunity guts its damage output.
- Trying to stay aggressive in melee while she's airborne instead of switching to the bow.
- Running out of arrows mid-fight from underestimating how ranged-heavy this fight is.
- Ignoring wolves or other Mountain wildlife that wander into the fight.
- Poor stamina management on the Mountain's uneven terrain, which can leave you unable to dodge when it matters.
