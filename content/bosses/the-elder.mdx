**The Elder is Valheim's second boss**, fought in [Black Forest](/world/regions/black-forest). It's the first fight that actually asks for preparation — and the first with a clear elemental weakness worth building around. See [how this site sources boss guides](/data-sources#boss-fight-guides).

## Quick facts

|---|---|
| Biome | [Black Forest](/world/regions/black-forest) |
| Summon item | 3 Ancient Seeds, burned in the altar's bowl |
| Altar | Sacrificial stone altar, found via a Vegvísir in a Burial Chamber |
| Drops | 1 Elder Trophy, 1 Swamp Key |

## The fight

Three attacks, all range-dependent:

- **Stomp** (melee): slow and easy to dodge, roll, or block — a Bronze Buckler handles it well. It also flattens nearby trees, which can crush you if you haven't cleared the area first.
- **Reaching vines** (ranged): the single most dangerous attack — a barrage or wide cone of whips fired from range. Break line of sight with cover the moment you see it start.
- **Root summon** (ground hazard): breakable root creatures erupt from the ground around you. Kill them quickly with an axe or just move off the spot.

**Fire is The Elder's confirmed weakness**, agreed on across every source — fire arrows are consistently called the fastest, cleanest kill. Physical weapons work fine too, just less efficiently. No source found a strong resistance to any other damage type.

## Prep checklist

- **Weapon:** a Finewood Bow with Fire Arrows is the most-recommended core loadout — bring a large reserve (150-200+ arrows). A Bronze Axe covers melee and clears roots quickly.
- **Shield:** a Bronze Buckler for blocking the stomp.
- **Armor:** full Bronze set, or a mobility-focused Troll Hide alternative.
- **Food:** Carrot Soup, Deer Stew, or Grilled Neck Tail, plus Queen's Jam as an all-rounder. Aim for a solid HP buffer — the vine attack can take a big chunk in one hit.
- **Potions:** Minor Healing Mead and Minor Stamina Mead if you've fermented them.
- **Arena prep:** placing campfires around the arena beforehand adds a slow passive fire-damage source against the boss, on top of keeping some early enemies away.

## Arena and terrain

The altar sits inside a ring of four indestructible stone pillars — the single most useful tactical feature in the fight, both for breaking vine line-of-sight and for safe stamina regen between exchanges. The real threat usually isn't The Elder himself but nearby Greydwarf spawner nests, Trolls, or Skeletons wandering in mid-fight — clearing the surrounding area before summoning is the most repeated piece of advice across every guide.

## Common mistakes

- Not clearing nearby Greydwarf nests first — a "harmless" nest can keep feeding extra enemies into an already dangerous fight.
- Bringing Bronze gear but no fire arrows — the weakness is significant enough to build the whole strategy around.
- Standing near trees during his stomp.
- Running out of stamina mid-dodge; The Elder doesn't despawn and will chase over long distances once summoned.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you summon The Elder in Valheim?

Burn three Ancient Seeds at The Elder's Forsaken altar in the Black Forest. Vegvísir runestones in Burial Chambers and ruined structures can reveal an altar location.

### Is The Elder weak to fire?

Yes. Fire Arrows are especially useful because they exploit that weakness while allowing you to keep distance from the boss's close-range stomp and root hazards.

### What does The Elder drop?

The Elder drops his trophy and the Swamp Key. The key opens Sunken Crypts in the Swamp, making the fight the main progression gate to reliable Scrap Iron.
