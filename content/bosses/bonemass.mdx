**Bonemass is Valheim's third boss**, fought in [Swamp](/world/regions/swamp). It's the poison fight — sources agree Poison Resistance Mead is close to mandatory here, and it's the first boss where damage type genuinely matters. See [how this site sources boss guides](/data-sources#boss-fight-guides).

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Biome | [Swamp](/world/regions/swamp) |
| Summon item | 10 Withered Bones |
| Altar | Forsaken Altar, found via a Vegvísir or Runestone |

## The fight

- **Melee swipe:** a long-reach arm swing that hits hard and inflicts poison on top of physical damage. Its force can stagger through a weak block, so shield/block power matters.
- **Body-chunk throw:** a slow, telegraphed lob that spawns waves of Skeletons and Blobs on impact — and leaves Bonemass briefly open to free hits during the windup.
- **Poison cloud:** a lingering AoE gas cloud, usually following the melee swipe. This is the most dangerous move — get well clear of it rather than trying to tank or roll through it.

**Weak to Blunt, resistant to Pierce and Slash** — this is the one point every source agrees on unanimously. Some guides also report Frost effectiveness, though that's not universally confirmed, so treat it as a secondary option rather than a sure thing.

A constant localized rainstorm runs through the whole fight, applying the Wet debuff (reduced stamina regen) on top of everything else.

## Prep checklist

- **Weapon:** Iron Mace, Iron Sledge, Stagbreaker, or a Club — anything Blunt. Frostner is a solid pick if you want to test the reported frost effectiveness.
- **Shield:** a high block-power shield (Iron tower or banded shield) for the melee swipe.
- **Armor:** full Iron-tier armor — Bronze is consistently flagged as insufficient.
- **Poison Resistance Mead:** treated as close to mandatory across every source. Bring multiple doses; fights can outlast one.
- **Healing:** Medium Healing Mead or better.
- **Food:** hearty combinations like Sausages, Grilled Neck Tails, Serpent Stew, or Queen's Jam.

## Arena and terrain

Swamp terrain around the altar is uneven and partly submerged — leveling the ground with a Hoe beforehand prevents getting stuck mid-fight. Leeches in nearby water and wandering Draugr, Wraiths, or Surtlings can interrupt the fight if the area isn't cleared first. The body-chunk throw is the only source of adds — Skeletons and Blobs only arrive through that specific attack, not a passive spawn.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping or letting Poison Resistance Mead expire mid-fight — the single most repeated warning.
- Bringing Pierce or Slash weapons instead of Blunt.
- Standing in the poison cloud instead of moving well clear of it.
- Fighting in unleveled, pitted swamp terrain instead of prepping the ground first.
- Letting spawned adds pile up instead of clearing them between exchanges.
