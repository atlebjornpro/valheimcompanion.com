**Released September 9, 2026.** Iron Gate's announcement marks the full release and arrival of Deep North. Expand its spoiler section for the complete named content list.

## What changed

- Deep North introduces Kall Fimbulbringer, new creatures, snow and ice mechanics, dungeons, materials, and food.
- Crafting additions include Frost Foundry, Frigid Kiln, Standing Loom, Smith's Aprons, and Smoker.
- Nord, Frostfire, and Thunderblood weapons join Protector, Vanguard, Caller, and Lox Fur armor.
- New systems include a Grappling Hook, inventory upgrades from Haldor, item upgrades at a hidden forge, cinematics, alternate earlier biomes, and achievements.
- Existing content receives Ashlands spawn adjustments, increased Mistlands and Ashlands gear durability, expanded mist removal, and revised building menus.

The [Deep North guide](/deep-north) separates named additions from unpublished recipes and combat data.

## Platform scope

The FAQ lists Steam on Windows, Linux and Mac; Mac App Store; Humble on Windows, Linux and Mac; Microsoft Store on Windows; Xbox One and Series X|S; PlayStation 5; and Nintendo Switch 2. Crossplay is supported across platforms.

## Dedicated servers and achievements

The notes describe a world-save system that divides data into chunks, world-generation and networking improvements, and IPv6 fixes. Preserve a complete backup before updating a server and test loading, saving, joining, and permission lists.

Achievements begin tracking with the installed release. Read the [achievement guide](/valheim-1-0/achievements-cheats) alongside the later [Steam hotfix](/patch-notes/v1-0-12), which changes the original cheat-lock policy.
