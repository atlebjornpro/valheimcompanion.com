**Released September 11, 2026.** Iron Gate labels this patch **1.0.10 on all platforms**, with **1.0.11 on Xbox** representing the same changes.

## What changed

Deep North heavy armor becomes repairable. The patch corrects Deep North Greydwarf Shaman damage and healing, changes how landing and punching affect snow, and revises creature and snow audio. No replacement combat values are provided.

## Platform scope

The announcement includes all platforms and reports restored crossplay after the Switch 2 patch delay. The separate [1.0.12 entry](/patch-notes/v1-0-12) covers that day's Steam-specific fixes.

## Dedicated servers and achievements

The 1.0.10 list contains no dedicated-server configuration or achievement-rule change. Server owners should update normally and test clients; different platform version labels alone do not establish incompatibility. Achievement opt-in belongs to the separately announced Steam patch.
